Blum hardware cost in Australia starts with small parts and adds up quickly: a published Blum LEGRABOX drawer kit starts at [about $233 including GST](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/drawer-kits/blum-legrabox), while a standard soft-close hinge starts near [about $14 including GST](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/hinges). In a NSW kitchen, that can mean the difference between a modest hinge-and-shelf fitout and several thousand dollars of deep drawers, lift doors and pull-outs. The useful question is not whether Blum is good. It is which moving parts deserve the spend.

## The short answer

Blum is a premium hardware choice, but “Blum hardware” is not one line item. It is hinges, drawers, lift systems, runners, internal organisers and sometimes motion technology. A kitchen with twenty doors and four drawers can use the brand without becoming a high-end hardware package. A kitchen with eight deep drawers, a bin pull-out, a pantry pull-out and two overhead lifts is another thing entirely.

Those are August 2026 retail starting points, not a quote and not a promise that every size is available at that price. Hardware is also only one share of a kitchen. Cabinetry commonly represents [about 35–45% of a kitchen renovation budget](https://www.leadkit.com.au/blog/kitchen-renovation-cost-sydney-2026), and an Australian kitchen renovation benchmark is [about $42,630](https://hackeraustralia.com.au/kitchen-renovation-costs-explained/). That is why comparing a $14 hinge with a $42,000 renovation can be misleading: the hinge is cheap; designing enough access to make the room work is not.

## The Blum range a joiner actually specifies

In the workshop, hardware starts with the opening action. A hinged door gives clear access to a shallow cupboard. A drawer gives access from above and keeps heavy pans from disappearing at the back. A lift door keeps an overhead door out of the cook’s face. The brand matters after that decision has been made.

Clip Top BLUMOTION hinges are the familiar quiet-close solution for hinged doors. They come in different overlays and opening angles because not every door sits in the same relationship to the carcass. A 155-degree hinge costs more than a basic 110-degree option because it solves a different clearance problem. That is a specification issue, not a showroom upgrade.

TANDEMBOX and LEGRABOX are metal-sided drawer families. LEGRABOX has a more refined slim side and more visible finish options, which matters on open drawers in a pared-back kitchen. The runner and drawer geometry need to match the cabinet width, depth, load and front height. We do not treat an organiser as an afterthought either: cutlery, plate and utensil systems must be selected while the drawer is designed, not forced in after the cabinetry is fitted.

AVENTOS is the lift-system family for overhead doors. It is useful above a coffee station, appliance garage or fridge where a conventional hinged door would sit in the way. It is not automatically better on every overhead cabinet. A simple hinged door often costs less, is easy to adjust and is entirely adequate for a low-use cupboard.

## Drawer systems, priced

A drawer quote should tell you how many drawer boxes, not merely say “soft-close drawers”. A full-depth pot drawer uses more material, runner capacity and cabinet work than a shallow cutlery drawer. Add an internal drawer behind a tall front and you gain clean lines but also another drawer box, another front and more fitting time.

| Drawer decision | Cost effect | Why it changes the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One LEGRABOX drawer kit | [From $233 including GST](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/drawer-kits/blum-legrabox) | Premium drawer box is only one component of an installed drawer |
| Replace shelves with deep drawers | Higher hardware and fabrication allowance | Better access to pans, plates and small appliances |
| Add inner drawers | Higher allowance per opening | Hides storage behind a clean tall front |
| Use wide, heavily loaded drawers | Higher allowance | Requires the right runner and stable cabinet construction |
| Keep a simple shelf cupboard | Lowest hardware allowance | Works where contents are light and rarely accessed |

This is where people waste money: specifying a premium drawer in a cabinet that could have been a simple shelf, then leaving the pot cupboard on shelves. Spend first on the drawers you open every day: under the cooktop, beside the dishwasher, at the coffee station and in the pantry. A single deeply useful drawer pays back in use; a decorative upgrade in a dead corner does not.

## Hinges and lift systems, priced

Hinges are usually the least dramatic hardware item until the kitchen is fitted. Then a door rubs, catches a pull, opens into another door or needs to clear a wall. The correct hinge resolves those conditions. The wrong hinge turns a good door front into a daily nuisance.

Published Australian retail examples show a regular full-overlay 110-degree Inserta or screw-on soft-close hinge at [about $14 including GST](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/hinges), a half-overlay version around [about $16](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/hinges), and a 155-degree full-overlay hinge from [about $37](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/hinges). Multiply by the hinge count per door, then remember that price does not include the door itself or drilling and hanging it. That multiplication is why a wall of small doors can cost more in hardware than a cleaner layout with fewer, well-proportioned fronts.

Lift systems should be quoted by product and opening size. They are useful above a microwave recess, a tea station or a high-use appliance shelf. They need correct door weight, height and adjustment. Do not accept a generic “lift hardware allowance” when the door dimensions have not been settled. That is how budget gaps arrive after approval.

## Inner dividers, organisers and bins

Internal organisation is where a good kitchen becomes easy to run. It is also a place where sales language gets loose. A drawer divider may be sensible in the top cutlery drawer and pointless in a junk drawer. A bin pull-out is valuable beside the preparation zone, not useful at the far end of the kitchen. Bottle storage and spice inserts work when the user has enough of those things to justify the lost flexible space.

I ask clients to list what sits on the bench because that list usually reveals the storage failure. If oils, knives, lunch boxes and charging cords all live in view, the answer is not necessarily more organisers. It may be one correctly sized drawer, a power point in an appliance cupboard or a deeper pantry shelf.

For [laundry cabinet storage](/laundry-cabinets), I would rather see a robust pull-out hamper or two properly sized drawers than an expensive internal accessory that traps damp items. In [built-in wardrobe hardware](/built-in-wardrobes), hanging length, shoe access and the ability to see folded clothes matter more than matching the kitchen’s drawer finish.

## Blum against Hettich, Grass and Titus

There is no universal winner. Blum, Hettich, Grass and Titus all make hardware families suited to different cabinetry and budgets. Availability, installer familiarity, the exact drawer system and the replacement path matter as much as the badge stamped on a runner.

| Brand family | Typical positioning | Good reason to specify it | Question to ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blum | Premium, broad drawer and lift ecosystem | Consistent system across drawers, hinges and lifts | Is the exact product named and scheduled? |
| Hettich | Broad residential and commercial hardware range | Alternative premium or mid-premium system | Can parts be supplied locally later? |
| Grass | Drawer and runner specialist | Refined drawer action where specified | Which runner and load rating is being used? |
| Titus | Practical hardware and damping solutions | Cost-sensitive applications or specific fittings | Does it suit the cabinet and frequency of use? |

We do not make an official-dealer claim for any brand in this guide. We specify hardware to the project. If a quote says only “Blum or equivalent”, ask what equivalent means. Is it an equivalent opening action, load capacity, adjustment range, finish or warranty? Those are not interchangeable.

## Where the premium is worth it, and where it is not

Premium hardware is worth it under the cooktop, in the main pantry, in a waste pull-out and in every drawer that carries plates, cookware or food each day. It is also worthwhile when the hardware must be accessed after the kitchen is complete. Replacing a runner inside a carefully finished pantry is more disruptive than choosing a suitable one from the outset.

It is less critical in a narrow overhead cupboard holding platters, a linen cupboard opened once a week, or a light-duty rental refresh where a durable standard hinge is enough. I would never push a whole-house premium package simply so a spec sheet looks impressive. Buy better access where it changes the way you live.

The same rule applies to finish. Dark drawer sides can look sharp under a timber veneer kitchen but add no storage. A functional internal drawer, bin pull-out or wide-angle hinge can solve an actual problem. Put the budget into the problem first.

## Warranty and what happens in year seven

A hardware warranty is a product commitment, and its terms are product- and supplier-specific. Keep the hardware schedule, invoice and adjustment information. A door can need adjustment after normal settlement, and a failed component is easier to identify if we know which system was installed.

For residential building work, NSW statutory warranties run for [six years for major defects and two years for other defects](https://hia.com.au/resources-and-advice/managing-your-business/managing-compliance/articles/statutory-warranties-for-home-building-work). That is a legal framework, not a reason to ignore manufacturer documents. In year seven, the practical question is whether the exact part can be identified, supplied and fitted without dismantling adjacent work. Named, common systems make that conversation easier.

SteepWood provides a 10-year structural warranty on all joinery. Product hardware terms remain separate, so they should be recorded with the handover information.

## Costing the hardware in a real 6 lm kitchen

Take a six-linear-metre kitchen with eight hinged doors, six drawer fronts, one pull-out bin and one overhead lift. A sensible brief starts by assigning locations: deep drawers below the cooktop, a bin by the preparation run, standard hinges where shelves work, then a lift only where a door would clash.

At published retail starting points, six LEGRABOX kits would begin at $1,398 before fronts, fabrication and fitting; eight standard 110-degree hinges would begin at $112 before installation. That is simple arithmetic from the [published drawer price](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/drawer-kits/blum-legrabox) and [hinge price](https://www.ekitchens.com.au/products/hardware-accessories/hinges), not an installed quote. It demonstrates why the schedule matters: drawers affect the cabinetry build, while hinges usually sit within a door package.

Now change the layout to ten drawers, an internal drawer pair, two lifts and a premium bin system. The room has not grown, but the hardware and manufacturing allowance has. ## FAQ

### How much does Blum hardware add to a kitchen?

It commonly adds hundreds to several thousand dollars, depending on the drawer count, lift doors, pull-outs and internal organisers. A hinge-only kitchen has a very different hardware allowance from a kitchen with deep LEGRABOX drawers, an AVENTOS lift and integrated bins. Ask for the hardware schedule, not a vague premium-hardware line.

### What does a LEGRABOX drawer cost in Australia?

A Blum LEGRABOX drawer kit starts at about $233 including GST at published Australian retail. That is a component starting point, not an installed drawer price: drawer fronts, cabinet machining, fitting, freight and configuration still need to be allowed for. Width, depth, internal drawer options and finish change the final allowance.

### Is Blum worth the extra money?

Blum is worth the extra money where a drawer is deep, heavily used or difficult to replace later. It is less compelling for a seldom-used shallow cupboard or a basic rental refresh. The sensible decision is to spend on the working drawers and corners first, then choose the remaining fittings to suit the budget.

### What is the warranty on Blum hardware?

Warranty terms depend on the exact Blum product and the supplier or installer providing it, so obtain the current written terms with your quote. Separately, NSW home-building statutory warranties run for six years for major defects and two years for other defects. Those legal rights are not a substitute for keeping product documents.

### Can I get Blum in a flat-pack kitchen?

Yes, some flat-pack systems and retrofit suppliers offer Blum-compatible or genuine Blum components. Check drilling patterns, drawer-box dimensions and whether the carcass has been designed for that hardware before buying it. Retrofitting a hinge can be straightforward; converting a drawer system can require new parts and careful cabinet machining.

### What is the difference between TANDEMBOX and LEGRABOX?

TANDEMBOX is a metal-sided drawer system intended for dependable everyday cabinetry, while LEGRABOX is the more refined, slimmer-sided system with a broader premium finish and organisation range. The right choice is not automatically the dearer one. The cabinet layout, load, visible side profile and budget should decide it.
