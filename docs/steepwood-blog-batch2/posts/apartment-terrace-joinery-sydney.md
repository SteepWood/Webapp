## The short answer

Treat apartment joinery as a building-coordination job with a furniture scale. Confirm the approval path first, take a complete measure second, then settle the cabinet layout. The common failure is allowing a beautiful design to go to manufacture before anyone has checked a loading dock, lift internal size, work hours or plumbing scope.

## Why an apartment job is not a small house job

A house has a front door, perhaps a side gate, a driveway and a relatively forgiving installation day. An apartment can have a concierge, a loading dock, a booked lift, a protective-cover requirement, neighbours immediately either side and a hard stop on work hours. The cabinetry may be shorter but the coordination is denser.

Apartment rooms also use every millimetre. A shallow pantry is not a failure if it has sensible internal pull-outs. A robe can be a good room divider if it allows enough passage and does not block light. A laundry cupboard needs more than doors around a machine: the appliance, hose, drainage, electrical and ventilation requirements need the right trades and approvals.

## Strata approval: the three categories of work and which applies to joinery

NSW guidance distinguishes cosmetic work, minor renovations and major renovations. The practical point for a joinery buyer is simple: [kitchen or bathroom renovations need permission](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/strata/living/renovations), while cosmetic changes that do not affect structure or require waterproofing may not. The right category depends on the actual scope, and your by-laws can add practical requirements.

| Broad category | Joinery example | Sensible first step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cosmetic work | Freestanding cabinet or painting | Read the scheme by-laws |
| Minor renovation | New robe or kitchen cabinets not involving major change | Obtain the written renovation process |
| Major or building work | Work affecting common property, structure or waterproofing | Obtain professional and owners-corporation advice |

Do not use this table as a legal determination. [NSW strata renovation guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/strata/living/renovations) and your scheme’s by-laws are the primary documents, and this article is general information, not legal advice. If a kitchen change touches a wet area, walls, floors, ceilings, common services or waterproofing, do not guess.

## What the owners corporation can and cannot refuse

An owners corporation administers the scheme’s by-laws and common property. It can ask for the information that the applicable process requires and can impose conditions that are consistent with the scheme rules. The [NSW Government’s by-law guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/strata/living/by-laws) makes clear that renovation rules commonly cover much more than the cabinetry itself: flooring, balconies, parking, noise and common-property use can all be relevant.

The practical answer is not to argue from a general blog post. Ask the strata manager for the renovation form, current by-laws, insurance and access requirements, then give the joiner a clean written brief. A complete application reduces the chance that the building manager finds out about the job after delivery has been booked.

## Building access: service lifts, loading docks, booking windows and trading hours

Access should sit on the joinery measure sheet. We record the loading address, vehicle restrictions, dock height, lift dimensions, lift booking duration, route from lift to unit, mats or wall protection, parking arrangement and permitted working window. A tall pantry that clears a room doorway may not clear a service lift. A stone top needs a separate handling plan from a lightweight overhead cabinet.

This is where small projects become inefficient. A crew can be ready, cabinets can be built, and the day still stops because the lift was not reserved. A good quote can allow for known constraints; it cannot price unknown restrictions accurately. Confirm them early and send the written conditions with the approved drawings.

## Noise, dust and the neighbours

Cutting, drilling and packaging removal are normal parts of installation, but apartment buildings make the impact more immediate. The building may impose work hours, lift bookings and dust-control expectations. A cabinet installation is not a demolition licence. If walls, plumbing, tiling, flooring or waterproofing are part of the project, programme them with the appropriate trades and building requirements rather than adding them informally on the day.

The cleanest sequence is approve, isolate the work area, protect the common route, complete the necessary trades, install cabinets, then template and fit any separately supplied benchtop. Each building has its own procedure. Ask for it before setting a date.

## Waterproofing, and the one mistake that becomes a claim

Cabinetry around a bathroom or laundry does not replace waterproofing. The mistake is assuming a new vanity, appliance cupboard or relocated laundry is only a joinery decision. Once a wet area’s waterproofing, plumbing or drainage is affected, it becomes a coordinated building scope. A cabinetmaker can make the enclosure fit accurately; licensed wet-area trades must deal with their own work and compliance.

That distinction protects both the client and the installer. It also stops a joinery quote from becoming an unclear promise about works it does not include. Put every trade, exclusion and existing-condition assumption in writing.

## Joinery that solves small apartment footprints

Small space joinery works best when it gives a room one clear job. A combined study and guest room may need a wall bed only if the structure, fixing and daily use justify it. A living room may need a low cabinet rather than a full wall. A one-bedroom apartment often gains more from a carefully planned robe and entry cupboard than from a marginally larger kitchen.

| Problem | Joinery response | Planning guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No entry storage | Shallow full-height cupboard | Measure door swing and circulation |
| Short kitchen run | Tall pantry plus deep drawers | Keep appliance ventilation clear |
| Laundry in cupboard | Stacked appliance enclosure | Confirm trade and ventilation requirements |
| Work-from-home corner | Demountable desk and shelving | Avoid blocking egress or light |
| Small bedroom | Floor-to-ceiling robe | Check door and bed clearance |

A 600 mm deep robe or bench is a common cabinet dimension. Do not turn it into a fixed room rule. The room’s openings, appliance model and circulation determine whether it fits.

## Freestanding and demountable joinery for renters and short holds

Freestanding pieces are sometimes the better answer. A solid cabinet, desk, sideboard or modular bookcase can be transported later and may avoid approvals associated with fixed alterations. It is useful where the lease is short, the owner does not want to alter the unit or the building’s process is disproportionate to the benefit.

The trade-off is fit. Freestanding furniture leaves gaps around irregular walls, skirtings and services. It can look right in an open living room and fail in a kitchen or a bedroom that needs every centimetre. ## FAQ

### Do I need strata approval for new joinery?

Usually, kitchen or bathroom renovation work needs permission in a NSW strata scheme, while purely cosmetic work may not. The applicable category and process depend on the work and the scheme's by-laws, so read the current by-laws and ask the owners corporation or strata manager before committing. This is general information, not legal advice.

### How much is an apartment kitchen renovation in Sydney?

A mid-range Sydney kitchen renovation is an indicative 2026 range of $30,000 to $55,000, but a compact apartment is not automatically cheap. Shorter cabinet runs can be offset by lift bookings, restricted hours, protective requirements and a high concentration of drawers, appliances and tall storage. Compare installation logistics as well as cabinet specification.

### Can you install joinery in a building with a service lift booking system?

Yes, provided the building's booking, protection, loading-dock and work-hour rules are known before manufacturing and delivery are booked. A site measure should include lift dimensions, travel route and any requirement for floor or wall protection. The owners corporation controls common-property access, so the building rules set the practical programme.

### What can an owners corporation refuse?

An owners corporation can apply its by-laws and require an approval pathway for work affecting common property, waterproofing, structure or the appearance and amenity of the scheme. It is not sensible to assume any particular request will be approved or refused without the scheme documents. Obtain the written requirements for your building; this is general information, not legal advice.

### Is freestanding joinery a good option for renters?

Freestanding joinery can be a good option for renters and short holds because it can provide storage without permanently fixing a full wall of cabinetry. The trade-off is lower capacity and less ability to bridge irregular walls, skirtings and services. Check the tenancy agreement and building rules before installing or anchoring any large item.

### Do apartment jobs cost more per metre?

They often can cost more per metre because a compact job still needs design, measure, transport, protection, access coordination and installation time. It is also more likely to contain expensive components such as integrated appliances, deep drawers and custom fillers. The total may be lower than a house kitchen, while the per-metre rate is higher.
