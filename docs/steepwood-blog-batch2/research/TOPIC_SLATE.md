# Batch 2 Topic Slate — 30 posts, scored

Evidence base: `research/NSW-MARKET-RESEARCH.md` (40 Australian SERPs, 34 competitor profiles, 52 NSW LGAs from ABS, 110+ compliance and cost pages).

Scoring key, each 1–5:

- **Intent** — commercial intent of the query family (5 = buyer with a budget, 1 = idle curiosity)
- **Winnability** — weakness of the incumbent page-one field (5 = broken SERP or no practitioner content, 1 = entrenched authority)
- **Value** — revenue per converted enquiry for SteepWood
- **Cannibalisation risk** — overlap with the six live Batch 1 posts and with the 10 service pages (5 = severe, 1 = none). Lower is better.
- **Priority score** = Intent + Winnability + Value − Cannibalisation risk. Max 14.
