# NSW Location Strategy — which places to own, and why

All demand figures are **value of alterations and additions approved, 2025-26 financial year** from the [ABS LGA building approvals data cube, June 2026](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/building-and-construction/building-approvals-australia/jun-2026/87310do005_202606.xlsx). Population and median weekly household income are ABS 2021 Census. Full 52-LGA table is in `NSW-MARKET-RESEARCH.md` §4a.

Why this metric: alterations and additions approvals are the closest public proxy for addressable custom-joinery renovation demand. Total dwelling approvals are tracked separately because they identify the new-build and second-fix channel, which behaves differently.

## 2. The hard constraint that shapes everything

Google states local results are ranked mainly on **relevance, distance and prominence**, and that prominence is partly driven by inbound links and by review volume and rating ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)). A service-area business may hold **only one profile**, may list **at most 20 service areas**, and **cannot** define a service area as a radius ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en)).

SteepWood's workshop is in Newcastle. Distance to a Woollahra or Northern Beaches searcher cannot be changed, and the Google Business Profile cannot be stretched to cover NSW. Therefore:

> Everything outside Greater Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, Port Stephens and the Central Coast must be won through **organic website relevance** — location landing pages and location-specific content — plus **prominence** (reviews, citations, links). Not through Google Business Profile geography.

This is why 11 of the 30 posts are location guides, and why every one of the other 19 carries a mandatory NSW location matrix section.

### Recommended allocation of the 20 Google Business Profile service-area slots

Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, Port Stephens, Cessnock, Central Coast, Wyong, Gosford, Singleton, Muswellbrook, Wollongong, Shellharbour, Kiama, Bowral, Sydney, Northern Beaches, Woollahra, Chatswood, Parramatta, The Hills Shire.

Rationale: the first ten are the drive-time catchment where distance actually helps. The last ten are the highest-value markets where an area listing at least makes SteepWood eligible to appear.

Newcastle plus Lake Macquarie plus Central Coast alone is $374.1m of approved alterations. The Central Coast is the **seventh-largest alterations market in NSW** and holds the **largest dwelling stock outside Sydney** at 152,706 dwellings.

Known SERP defect to exploit: for *custom joinery Newcastle*, **five of ten page-one results are UK or US businesses** (`ajsjoinery.co.uk`, `jpcjoinery.co.uk`, `jarvisgreenjoinery.co.uk`, `specialistjoineryco.co.uk`, `west-end-cabinet.com`). For *joinery Newcastle NSW*, a directory (Localsearch) outranks every joiner. Aggressive geo-disambiguation — "Newcastle NSW 2300", "the Hunter", postcode and LGA naming, ABN, Licence 489553C, `areaServed` schema — is a structural fix nobody else has made.

Keyword evidence: *kitchen renovations newcastle* 210 searches per month, KD 21, CPC $2.02; *kitchen renovations central coast* 170 per month, **KD 8** ([keyword research PDF](https://cdn2.f-cdn.com/files/download/193561461/Keyword%20Research%20For%20Brilliant%20Australia%20-%20Sheet1.pdf)).

Posts: 20 (Newcastle and Hunter), 21 (Central Coast).
