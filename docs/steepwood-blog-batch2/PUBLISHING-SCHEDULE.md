# Publishing Schedule — 30 posts, one per day

Window: **Saturday 22 August 2026 → Sunday 20 September 2026**, inclusive. Thirty consecutive calendar days, one post per day, no gaps.

Timezone for all publishing: **Australia/Sydney (AEST, UTC+10)**. Recommended publish time **07:00 AEST**, which is the start of the workshop day and ahead of the morning research peak.

Seasonal logic: spring begins 1 September in Australia and spring is the busiest renovation season in the country, with tradespeople booking out weeks ahead ([ServiceSeeking](https://www.serviceseeking.com.au/industry-insights/is-spring-a-good-time-to-renovate-7-reasons)). The ten posts published 22–31 August are therefore the cost and lead-time content a homeowner reads while deciding whether to book before spring. The Sydney and regional location guides land in the second half, once the commercial and materials cluster has given them internal links to receive.

## Internal linking rule — read this before writing any post

All 30 files are committed at once, but they go live one per day. A link from day 4 to day 22 would be a 404 for 18 days, which wastes crawl budget and harms the reader.

**Rule: a post may only link to a Batch 2 post with an earlier publish date than its own.** Every post may always link to the six live Batch 1 posts, to the 10 service pages, to the 16 existing location hubs, and to `/quote`, `/contact`, `/about`, `/portfolio` and `/legal/consumer-rights`.

Per-post allowed Batch 2 outbound targets are listed in `research/POST-BRIEFS.md`. Each post carries **6 to 10 internal links** total: at least two service pages, at least one location page, at least one Batch 1 post, and one `/quote` call to action.

**Backfill pass, day 31.** After 20 September, run one editing pass adding forward links: each post from day 1 to day 29 gains two to three links to later posts in its own cluster. This is the single highest-leverage post-launch task and it is specified in `cursor/CURSOR_PROMPT-PHASE1.md`.

## Cadence and promotion checklist per post

On the publish day for each post:

1. Confirm the post renders at `/blog/{slug}` with hero, OG image, breadcrumb, reading time and author aside intact.
2. Confirm `BlogPosting` plus `FAQPage` plus `BreadcrumbList` schema validates. `HowTo` and `Product` where the brief specifies them. See `cursor/SCHEMA-SPEC.md`.
3. Confirm the post appears in `sitemap.xml` with the correct `lastmod`.
4. Request indexing in Google Search Console for the new URL.
5. Publish a Google Business Profile update post linking to the article, with the primary location named. Local guides get the matching service-area mention.
6. Add the post to the newsletter queue for the Friday send.
7. Log the target keyword in the rank tracker with the publish date so movement can be attributed.

Thirteen reviews is the single weakest local ranking input in the whole account.
