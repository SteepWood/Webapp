It has to hide cables, hold files and books, suit a Federation home in Wahroonga or a new apartment in Chatswood, and still look like part of the house when the laptop is closed. This guide covers costs, layouts, materials and how to choose a joiner, for homes from Lane Cove and Mosman to Manly and Dee Why.

The North Shore and Northern Beaches spend more on renovations than almost anywhere in NSW. ## Getting the ergonomics right

A built-in desk is fixed, so the height has to be right from day one.

- **Desk height.** Desky's calculator puts a comfortable [desk height for most adults at about 635 to 700 mm](https://desky.com.au/pages/desk-height-calculator).
- **Leg room.** ErgoExperts recommends at least [650 mm of clear knee width](https://ergoexperts.com.au/what-is-the-correct-desk-size/).
- **Desk depth.** 600 mm suits a laptop and one screen. Go deeper for two screens.
- **Monitor arms.** Plan a solid fixing point in the desk top.
- **Lighting.** Put the desk side-on to the window to avoid glare on the screen.

## Materials and finishes

- **Timber veneer.** Oak or blackbutt veneer suits period homes in Wahroonga, Turramurra and Killara.

- **Cable management.** Grommets, cable trays and a power rail under the desk.

## How North Shore joiners compare

Local workshops include North Shore Design and Joinery in Asquith and Creative Cabinetry in Manly Vale, and Sydney-wide firms such as Golden Touch Joinery, which says [manufacture takes one to three weeks and installation about a day](https://www.goldentouchjoinery.com.au/home/home-office-designs). When comparing quotes, check:

- **Licence and insurance.** Residential work over $5,000 needs a licence and work over $20,000 needs HBCF cover, as [Renova Build explains](https://renovabuild.com.au/pages/commercial-fit-outs-sydney-renova-build). See our [NSW licence and HBCF guide](/blog/nsw-building-licence-hbcf-warranty-guide).
- **Board and hardware.** Ask for the board brand and hinge and runner brand in writing.
- **Install team.** Ask who installs and whether they are the same people who built it.
- **Warranty.** A structural warranty on carcasses gives more protection than a short workmanship promise.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does home office joinery cost on the North Shore?

Published Sydney ranges are about $3,000 to $8,000 for a built-in desk with storage and $8,000 to $18,000 for a full home office wall. North Shore homes with heritage details or strata access can sit higher. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### How long does it take to build a home office?

Manufacture usually takes a few weeks after you approve the drawings. Installation of a single wall of joinery is often one or two days on site.

### Do I need a licensed joiner for a built-in study?

In NSW, residential building work over $5,000 needs a licensed contractor, and work over $20,000 needs Home Building Compensation Fund cover. Check the licence on the Service NSW register.

### Can you fit a home office in a North Shore apartment?

Yes. Fold-away desks, wall-hung units and joinery in a spare bedroom or hallway all work well. Strata rules for work hours, lifts and parking need to be booked in advance.

### Do you work on the Northern Beaches as well?

Yes. We install from Manly and Dee Why to Mona Vale, as well as across the Lower and Upper North Shore.
