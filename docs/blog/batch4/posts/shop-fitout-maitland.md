## Approvals for Maitland shop fitouts

Most retail fitouts in NSW go through one of three paths.

- **Exempt work.** Minor internal changes that do not affect structure, fire safety or use.
- **Complying development.** Many shop fitouts qualify for a complying development certificate through a private certifier. AcroCert says a [CDC for shop fitouts can be issued in about five to ten working days](https://www.acrocert.com.au/shop-and-office-fitouts) when the plans comply.
- **Development application.** Needed for change of use, heritage items, food premises with new exhaust and many shopfront changes.

Central Maitland's High Street and the heritage parts of Morpeth and West Maitland have many listed and older buildings. Check heritage status before you change a shopfront, signage or internal walls.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Ordering joinery before centre or landlord approval.
- Ignoring heritage rules on older High Street buildings.
- Leaving power and data positions open.
- Painted finishes on counters that take heavy wear.
- No allowance in the budget for after-hours work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a shop fitout cost in Maitland?

Published NSW retail ranges are about $1,000 to $1,500 per square metre for a basic fitout, $1,500 to $2,500 for mid-range and $2,500 to $4,000 or more for premium. A 60 square metre Maitland shop at mid-range could be $90,000 to $150,000. These are planning figures, not a quote.

### Do I need council approval for a shop fitout in Maitland?

Many retail fitouts can be approved as complying development through a private certifier, sometimes in five to ten working days. Heritage buildings, change of use and food premises usually need more. Confirm the pathway before you design.

### Can you work in Stockland Green Hills?

Yes. Centre tenancies need design approval from centre management, inductions, insurance certificates and usually after-hours work. Send us the tenancy design manual early so the joinery is designed to it.

### Do you do shop fitouts in Cessnock and Kurri Kurri?

Yes. We install across the Hunter Valley, including Cessnock, Kurri Kurri, Rutherford, Singleton and the vineyard cellar doors.

### How long does a Maitland shop fitout take?

Once design and approval are done, a small shop is often two to four weeks on site. Joinery is built in our Newcastle workshop while the site works happen, so the closure period stays short.
