## Using incentives to fund the fitout

In a high-vacancy market, the incentive is often worth more than the rent discount you might negotiate on the face rate.

- **Ask early.** Raise the fitout contribution before heads of agreement, not after.
- **Get the split in writing.** Rent-free months and fitout cash are not the same. Cash up front pays your contractors.
- **Check the make-good clause.** Custom joinery you install may need to be removed at the end of the lease. Agree what stays.
- **Consider B Grade space with a strong fitout.** With B Grade vacancy near 40 per cent, a well-fitted B Grade floor can cost far less than an A Grade tower and still impress clients.
- **Use existing services.** Reusing ceilings, lighting and air conditioning keeps more of the incentive for joinery and finishes.

## Timeline from lease to move-in

Fitout timelines in Parramatta follow the same pattern as the rest of Sydney. Premier Office Solutions quotes [two to six weeks on site](https://www.premierofficesolutions.com.au/location/parramatta/) for simpler projects.

1. Brief and test-fit plan, one to two weeks.
2. Design development and pricing, two to four weeks.
3. Landlord approval and certification, two to four weeks.
4. Joinery shop drawings and workshop manufacture, running in parallel with approvals.
5. Construction on site, two to eight weeks depending on scope.
6. Defects, cleaning and move-in.

## Common Parramatta fitout mistakes

- Signing the lease before the fitout contribution is agreed.
- Designing for today's headcount, with no allowance for hybrid days.
- Leaving power and data positions open until after joinery is ordered.
- Choosing painted finishes for counters and kitchenette doors that take heavy wear.
- Forgetting tower rules for deliveries, lifts and noisy work hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an office fitout cost in Parramatta?

Published 2026 guides put a standard office fitout around $500 to $1,500 per square metre, with premium fitouts higher. A 200 square metre office could land anywhere from about $100,000 to $300,000 depending on partitions, services and joinery. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### Will a Parramatta landlord pay for my fitout?

Often in part. Cushman and Wakefield reported average gross incentives of about 34 per cent in Parramatta in Q3 2026. Incentives can be taken as rent-free time or as a fitout contribution, so negotiate the split before you design.

### How long does a Parramatta office fitout take?

A small, simple fitout can be on site for two to six weeks. Larger fitouts with new services, glazing and joinery often take eight to twelve weeks once design and approvals are done.

### Do I need approval for an office fitout in Parramatta?

Many internal office fitouts can go through complying development with a private certifier, but it depends on the building, fire services and the scope. Your landlord will also need to approve the drawings.
