# Pharmacy Fitout NSW: Costs, Pharmacy Council Rules and Joinery

A pharmacy fitout NSW owners plan in 2026 has two jobs. It must sell front-of-shop products like any retail store, and it must meet the premises rules that the Pharmacy Council of NSW inspects before a pharmacy can open. This guide covers costs, the regulation, dispensary and consult room design, the joinery list and the approval steps, for pharmacies in Sydney, Newcastle, the Central Coast and regional NSW.

## The NSW premises rules

The standards sit in the [Health Practitioner Regulation (New South Wales) Regulation 2025](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/pdf/inforce/2025-09-25/sl-2025-0430). For a community pharmacy, the premises must have:

- At least one doorway allowing direct public access.
- Secure doors, windows, floors and ceilings to protect scheduled medicines.
- A dispensing area of at least 8 square metres, unless the Pharmacy Council approves otherwise.
- A part of the premises where a pharmacist consultation is not reasonably likely to be overheard.
- The equipment and publications listed in the regulation.

The dispensing area itself must be adequately lit and ventilated, have a stainless steel or similar impervious sink with hot and cold water, have a bench at least 40 cm wide giving at least 1 square metre of free working space with an impervious covering, and have a barcode scanner at each dispensing station.

The [Health Professional Councils Authority](https://www.hpca.nsw.gov.au/councils/pharmacy/how-we-regulate-pharmacy-businesses) says the Council inspects premises before authorising any new pharmacy, and the regulation includes items such as a vaccine refrigerator and a private consultation area.

## What your plans must show

The [Pharmacy Council guide](https://www.hpca.nsw.gov.au/resources/guide) lists what premises plans must include. Joinery affects most of it:

- The dispensary perimeter, marked to separate it from the rest of the pharmacy.
- The total dispensary area in square metres.
- The location of dispensing benches, compounding benches and the sink.
- Vaccine refrigerators and dispensing computer stations.
- Shelving, including carousel units and robotic dispensers.
- Treatment, vaccination and consulting rooms.

Approval and inspection are also needed before you relocate, renovate, expand or reduce the size of approved premises, as the [Pharmacy Council](https://www.pharmacycouncil.nsw.gov.au/have-you-outgrown-%E2%80%9Capproved%E2%80%9D-premises-your-pharmacy-business?1747986512) reminds owners. Fees apply; check the current [Pharmacy Council fees](https://www.hpca.nsw.gov.au/councils/pharmacy/fees-and-charges) before you lodge.

## Design tips for a working dispensary

- **Plan the workflow.** Script in, dispense, check, counsel, collect. Joinery should follow that path.
- **Line of sight.** The pharmacist should be able to see the counter and consult room door from the dispensary.
- **Allow for robots and carousels.** If automation is likely within the lease, leave space and services now.
- **Drawer sizing.** Size drawers to your packs, not to a standard cabinet module.
- **Durability.** Dispensary drawers open thousands of times a week. Choose runners rated for it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a pharmacy fitout cost in NSW?

Published 2026 guides put pharmacy fitouts at about $2,000 to $3,500 per square metre, so a 150 square metre pharmacy may be $300,000 to $450,000. Small refits can start near $80,000 and large pharmacies can exceed $600,000. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### How big does a dispensary need to be in NSW?

The NSW regulation requires a dispensing area of at least 8 square metres unless the Pharmacy Council of NSW approves otherwise, with a bench at least 40 cm wide giving at least 1 square metre of free working space.

### Does the Pharmacy Council need to approve my fitout?

Yes. The Council inspects premises before a new pharmacy can operate, and approval is needed before relocating, renovating, expanding or reducing the size of approved premises. Submit plans that show the dispensary, its area and its layout.

### Do I need a private consultation room?

The regulation requires a part of the premises where a pharmacist consultation is not reasonably likely to be overheard. Most new pharmacies build an enclosed consult or vaccination room to meet this and to support services.

### What joinery does a pharmacy need?

Dispensary benches and drawers, script-in and collection counters, front-of-shop gondolas and wall displays, a consult room, a lockable storage area and staff facilities.
