## Planning for lakeside homes

Lake Macquarie homes have a few local quirks worth planning for.

- **Humidity near the water.** Waterfront homes at Belmont, Warners Bay, Toronto and Swansea see more moisture. Use moisture-resistant board in all wet areas, seal every cut edge and choose quality hinges and runners.
- **Older brick and fibro homes.** Many post-war homes in Charlestown, Cardiff and Windale have walls that are out of square. Allow for scribing and fillers in the design.
- **Asbestos in older homes.** Homes built before 1990 may contain asbestos behind old kitchens. [SafeWork NSW](https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/hazards-a-z/asbestos) explains when licensed removal is needed.
- **Flood-affected lots.** If you are extending or raising floors, check [Lake Macquarie City Council's flood information](https://www.lakemac.com.au/Development/Planning-controls-and-development-guidelines/Development-in-flood-affected-areas) first. A like-for-like kitchen replacement usually does not change floor levels.
- **Benchtop choice.** [Engineered stone is banned](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/silica/engineered-stone-ban), so plan for porcelain, sintered stone, natural stone or timber. See our [benchtop guide after the engineered stone ban](/blog/benchtop-guide-engineered-stone-ban-nsw).

## How to compare Lake Macquarie installers

- **Check the licence.** Use the [Service NSW licence check](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/check-a-builder-or-tradesperson-licence) and confirm the name on the quote matches.
- **Compare inclusions, not totals.** Some quotes include benchtops, removal and installation. Others do not.
- **Ask who installs.** Your kitchen should be fitted by the team that built it, or by installers the maker supervises.
- **Ask about board and hardware.** Moisture-resistant board and name-brand hardware cost a little more but last much longer.
- **Ask about warranty.** A structural warranty on cabinetry is worth more than a short workmanship promise.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does kitchen installation cost in Lake Macquarie?

Cabinet installation alone is commonly $1,800 to $4,500. A full custom kitchen in NSW commonly lands around $30,000 to $55,000 for a mid-range job, with larger or premium kitchens higher. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### How long does a Lake Macquarie kitchen installation take?

Manufacture usually takes four to eight weeks after you sign off drawings. Cabinet installation is often two to four days, then benchtops are templated and fitted about one to two weeks later.

### Do you install in Morisset and Toronto as well as Charlestown?

Yes. We install across the Lake Macquarie council area, from Charlestown, Warners Bay and Belmont to Toronto, Morisset, Swansea and Cooranbong, from our Newcastle workshop.

### What board should I use near the lake?

Moisture-resistant board for all wet-area carcasses is a sensible baseline, especially in waterfront homes with higher humidity. Pair it with sealed edges and quality hinges and runners.

### Can I still have stone benchtops?

Engineered stone has been banned in Australia since 1 July 2024. Porcelain, sintered stone, natural stone, compact laminate and timber are the common alternatives.
