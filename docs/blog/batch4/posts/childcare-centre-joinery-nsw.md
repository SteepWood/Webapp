# Childcare Centre Joinery NSW: Fitout Costs, Rules and Design

Childcare centre joinery NSW operators order in 2026 has to do three things at once. It must meet the Education and Care Services National Regulations, survive years of daily use by children and educators, and help a centre feel warm enough for parents to choose it. This guide covers the rules that affect joinery, the joinery list, costs and child-safe design for long day care and early learning centres in Sydney, Newcastle, the Central Coast and the Hunter.

SteepWood builds commercial joinery in our Newcastle workshop and installs across NSW, either directly for operators or as the joinery subcontractor to fitout builders.

## The rules that shape childcare joinery

The [Education and Care Services National Regulations](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-05/National%20regs%202%20January%202026.pdf), published by ACECQA, set the physical environment standards for centre-based services. Several directly affect joinery.

- **Regulation 112, nappy change.** If any children are under 3, the centre needs at least one properly constructed nappy change bench, adult hand cleansing facilities in the immediate vicinity, and facilities designed and located to prevent unsupervised access by children.
- **Regulation 111, administrative space.** The [same regulations](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-05/National%20regs%202%20January%202026.pdf) require adequate areas for administration, consulting with parents and private conversations.
- **Regulation 115, supervision.** The regulations require premises, including toilets and nappy change areas, to be designed and maintained so that children can be supervised at all times.

Your architect, certifier and the regulatory authority will confirm what applies to your service. The joinery has to be drawn to suit.

## Child-safe joinery design

[BPS Sydney's childcare guide](https://www.buildingprojectsolutionssydney.com/blog/childcare-fitout-cost-sydney-2026) lists child-safe joinery requirements: no sharp edges or corners within reach of children, child-safe hardware, non-toxic finishes and age-appropriate fixture heights. In practice:

- **Round every edge and corner** below about 1.2 metres.
- **Avoid finger traps.** Use soft-close hinges and leave safe gaps.
- **Fix tall units to the wall.** Nothing a child can climb should be able to tip.
- **Lock what needs locking.** Cleaning products, medicines and staff items go in lockable cupboards.
- **Choose low-emission board and finishes.** Ask for E0 or equivalent board.
- **Design for supervision.** Keep sight lines open over low joinery in playrooms and toward the nappy change area.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a childcare fitout cost in NSW?

Published 2026 guides put the internal fitout of an existing tenancy at about $1,800 to $4,000 or more per square metre. A renovation of an existing building for childcare commonly runs $400,000 to $1,500,000. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### What do the regulations say about nappy change areas?

Regulation 112 of the Education and Care Services National Regulations requires at least one properly constructed nappy change bench if any children are under 3, hand cleansing facilities for adults nearby, and facilities designed to prevent unsupervised access by children.

### What joinery does a childcare centre need?

Usually children's cubbies and bag lockers, playroom storage, nappy change benches with steps, a commercial kitchen, a staff room, reception and office joinery, a laundry and sleep room storage.

### What makes joinery child-safe?

Rounded edges and corners within reach of children, child-safe hardware, non-toxic finishes, finger-safe gaps and heights suited to the age group. Fix tall units to the wall.

### How long does childcare joinery take to make?

Joinery is often one of the longest lead items in a childcare fitout, so order it early. Fitout construction is commonly 8 to 16 weeks, and the joinery is manufactured during the early site works.
