# Warehouse Office Fitout Western Sydney: Costs, Mezzanines and Joinery

A warehouse office fitout Western Sydney businesses need in 2026 is usually a practical job: a front office for sales and admin, a lunchroom and lockers for the warehouse team, and somewhere decent to meet clients. This guide covers mezzanine and ground-floor costs, fire separation, approvals and the joinery that makes an industrial office work, from Blacktown and Eastern Creek to Wetherill Park, Smithfield, Penrith and Liverpool.

## Mezzanine or ground floor

| Factor | Mezzanine office | Ground-floor office |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per m² | Higher | Lower |
| Warehouse floor | Stays free for racking | Uses floor space |
| Access | Stairs, possibly a lift for accessibility | Easy access for visitors |
| Natural light | Often better, with high windows | Often limited |
| Height needed | Around 6 m clear is a common guide | No extra height needed |

[Stemar's planning guide](https://www.stemargroup.com.au/insights/warehouse-office-fitout-planning-guide) suggests about 6 metres clear height as a starting point for a mezzanine office, and notes that offices and warehouses are different building classes, so fire separation is commonly needed. Many businesses put reception and meeting rooms on the ground floor and admin on the mezzanine.

## Approvals and compliance

- **Building class.** Offices are Class 5 and warehouses are Class 7b under the National Construction Code. Mixing them in one building brings fire and egress requirements.
- **Complying development.** Many warehouse office and amenity fitouts can go through a CDC. One Sydney design firm quotes [about 3 to 6 weeks of design plus 2 to 4 weeks for a CDC](https://www.gianta.com.au/interior-fitout-design-sydney) for Class 7 and 8 warehouse office work.
- **Structure.** New mezzanines need engineering. Existing mezzanines may need checking before you add office loads.
- **Accessibility.** Visitor areas and toilets need accessible design.
- **Landlord approval.** Most industrial leases require landlord sign-off on drawings.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a warehouse office fitout cost in Western Sydney?

A published 2026 guide puts mezzanine offices at about $800 to $1,500 per square metre and ground-floor warehouse offices at about $600 to $1,200 per square metre, with typical projects around $80,000 to $350,000. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### Is a mezzanine office cheaper than building on the ground floor?

Not per square metre, because you pay for the structure, stairs and fire separation. But a mezzanine keeps the warehouse floor free for racking and forklifts, which is often worth more than the extra cost.

### What ceiling height do I need for a mezzanine office?

Planning guides commonly suggest a clear warehouse height of around 6 metres or more to fit a usable office above and working space below. Your designer and certifier will confirm what works for your building.

### Do warehouse offices need fire separation?

Often. Offices are a different building class from warehouses, so fire separation, egress paths and fire services are common requirements. Your certifier will set them for your building.
