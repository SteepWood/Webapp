## Approvals around the lake

Lake Macquarie has more approval layers than most NSW council areas. Check each one before you design.

### Exempt development

Under NSW planning rules, a deck can be exempt if it is [no more than 25 square metres, no more than 1 metre above existing ground and set back at least 900 mm from side and rear boundaries in most zones](https://planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/development-assessment/planning-approval-pathways/development-types/balconies-decks-and-patios). Exempt rules do not apply on some land, including many foreshore and flood-affected lots.

### Foreshore lots

[Lake Macquarie City Council's foreshore and waterway guidelines](https://www.lakemac.com.au/files/assets/public/v/1/hptrim/project-management-environmental-management-pm130197-foreshore-and-waterway-development-guidelines/pm130197-designs-and-plans-foreshore-development-guidelines/foreshore-and-waterway-development-guidelines-13-may-2024.pdf) state that a development application is required for all foreshore and waterway development. Work below the high water mark also needs Crown Lands consent.

### Flood-affected land

Some lakeside lots are flood-prone or affected by tidal inundation. Council's [flood information page](https://www.lakemac.com.au/Development/Planning-controls-and-development-guidelines/Development-in-flood-affected-areas) explains how to get a free flood report for your property.

### Mine subsidence

Much of Lake Macquarie sits in a [declared mine subsidence district](https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/subsidence-advisory/mine-subsidence-districts), including the Lake Macquarie, West Lake and Swansea North Entrance districts. Subsidence Advisory NSW approval is generally needed before building or extending a structure in a district. Check your address on the Spatial Viewer first.

## Choosing timber for a lakeside deck

| Option | Strengths | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spotted Gum | Very hard, [bushfire-resisting up to BAL-29](https://www.woodsolutions.com.au/publications/domestic-timber-deck-design) | Heavier, needs pre-drilling |
| Blackbutt | Pale colour, also rated up to BAL-29 | Shows dirt more on light boards |
| Merbau | Rich colour, stable | Tannin bleed onto paving early on |
| Composite | Low maintenance | Hotter underfoot in summer sun |
| Treated pine subframe | Cost-effective structure | Use H3 or H4 to suit exposure |

Near the water, use stainless steel screws and fixings, leave airflow under the boards and allow for regular oiling of hardwood. ## How to compare Lake Macquarie deck builders

- **Check the licence and its class.** Deck work is licensed in NSW, and the right licence depends on the size and type of deck. See the [NSW carpentry work licence page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/carpentry-work) and check names on the Service NSW register.
- **Ask who handles approvals.** A good builder will tell you early if you need a DA, a CDC or Subsidence Advisory approval.
- **Compare like with like.** Timber species, board width, fixings, stairs, balustrade and oil should all be listed.
- **Ask about footings.** Sloping and lakeside blocks often need deeper footings.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a deck cost in Lake Macquarie?

Local builders publish ranges of about $200 to $700 per square metre depending on height, timber and access. A small 4 by 4 metre hardwood deck has been quoted around $20,000 to $30,000 in the Newcastle region, and multi-level decks start around $25,000 to $30,000. These are planning ranges, not a quote.

### Do I need approval for a deck in Lake Macquarie?

A small, low deck can be exempt development under NSW rules if it is no more than 25 square metres, no more than 1 metre above ground and set back at least 900 mm in most zones. Foreshore lots, flood-prone land, heritage items and bushfire-prone land usually need approval.

### Is my Lake Macquarie property in a mine subsidence district?

Many suburbs are, including Charlestown, Cardiff, Warners Bay, Belmont, Toronto and Morisset. Check your address on the NSW Planning Portal Spatial Viewer. New structures in a district usually need Subsidence Advisory NSW approval.

### What is the best timber for a deck near the lake?

Spotted Gum and Blackbutt are durable class 1 above ground and suit bushfire-prone areas up to BAL-29. Use stainless steel fixings near the water, and leave good airflow under the deck.

### Can I build a deck right to the waterline?

Generally no. Development between the foreshore building line and the lake is limited and needs a development application. Anything below the high water mark, such as a jetty, also needs Crown Lands consent.
