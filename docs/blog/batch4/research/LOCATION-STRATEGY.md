## Google Business Profile constraint

SteepWood has one GBP, at the Newcastle workshop. Proximity favours Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and Maitland in the Maps pack. For Sydney sub-markets the blog posts compete in organic results, not the Maps pack. Do not create new GBP listings or virtual offices for these areas.
