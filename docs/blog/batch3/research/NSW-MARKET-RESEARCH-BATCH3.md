# NSW Market Research — Batch 3 (Fitout, Shopfitting, Commercial Joinery, Kitchen Installation, Decking, Home Office)

Prepared 6 November 2026 for SteepWood (Pavit Cabinetry Pty Ltd t/as SteepWood), Newcastle NSW. Window: 30 posts, originally 7 November to 6 December 2026; now all published 6 November 2026, dated 24 August to 6 November 2026.

Purpose: give Cursor and the owner the evidence behind the 30 topics, the keywords, the locations and the structure. Batch 2's evidence base (`blog-batch2/research/NSW-MARKET-RESEARCH.md`, `LOCATION-STRATEGY.md`) still applies and is not repeated here.

---

## 1. Summary

1. **The target services are commercial-led.** Office fitout, shopfitting, commercial joinery, kitchen installation, decking and home office joinery. Batch 1 and 2 were residential cost guides. Batch 3 fills the commercial and installation gap.
2. **Competitors in Newcastle and on the Central Coast run service pages, not content.** Most rivals have one page per service or suburb and few published cost guides with local tables. That is the gap.
3. **SteepWood already ranks for several local service pages** (`/office-fitout/newcastle`, `/shopfitting/central-coast`, `/office-fitout/central-coast`, `/home-office-joinery/newcastle`). Blog posts feed these pages with links rather than competing with them.
4. **Decking is a new category for the site.** Search results are led by exact-match local domains and directories. A joinery-led deck business with timber species knowledge is unusual in those results.
5. **Directories (hipages, Localsearch, wordofmouth) take several top spots** for Central Coast and Newcastle trade searches. Blog content plus Google Business Profile (GBP) activity is the way to get past them.

## 5. Keyword strategy

Keyword selection rules:

1. **Primary keyword = service + place or service + modifier.** Examples: "shop fitout Newcastle", "kitchen installation cost NSW".
2. **Primary keyword appears in the title, H1, first 100 words, meta description and URL slug.** Checked by `scripts/build_and_validate.py`.
3. **Secondary keywords carry suburb variants** (Gosford, Erina, Tuggerah, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, Parramatta).
4. **No two posts share a primary keyword.** The cross-batch check is in `KEYWORD-MAP.md`.
5. **Question-form keywords for informational posts** ("how long does kitchen installation take") to target People Also Ask boxes and featured snippets, backed by FAQPage schema.

The full map is in `../KEYWORD-MAP.md`.

## 6. Content structure standard

Every post follows the same structure, so it is consistent for readers and easy to parse for search engines:

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| H1 | One, matching the title |
| Intro | Primary keyword in the first 100 words, plus the regions served |
| H2s | At least 6, written as the questions searchers ask |
| Tables | At least 2 (costs, comparisons, regions) |
| Sources | At least 4 inline external sources on cost and market claims |
| CTA | One `> **STEEPWOOD ...` blockquote mid-article and a closing CTA |
| FAQ | 5 Q&As, rendered visibly and marked up as FAQPage |
| Trust line | ABN, licence, public liability, warranty |
| Internal links | Service page, location page, 2–4 related posts, `/quote` |
| Length | 1,000–1,900 words |
