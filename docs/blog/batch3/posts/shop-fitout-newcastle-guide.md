The counter has to work for staff, the shelving has to carry stock, and the whole thing has to be finished before your rent-free period runs out. ## The joinery that makes a shop work

Joinery is what customers see and staff use all day. List each item on the brief and ask for a separate price.

| Joinery item | What to specify | Typical material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service counter | Length, POS position, cash drawer, bag shelf, accessible section | Laminex or Polytec, timber veneer, stone or compact laminate top |
| Wall display shelving | Shelf loads, adjustability, lighting | Melamine or 2-pac with slotted or recessed shelf supports |
| Feature display | Height, movability, product weight | Timber, veneer or painted MDF |
| Stockroom shelving | Shelf depth, load, carton sizes | Industrial-grade board or steel |
| Fitting rooms | Number, door or curtain, mirror, hooks | Laminate panels, durable edging |

Good counters are designed around the staff workflow. Where does the EFTPOS sit, where do bags go, where does the till drawer open? We draw this in the shop drawings so it is agreed before manufacture.

## Landlord and shopping centre rules

Most centres, including the larger Hunter shopping centres, issue a tenancy design manual. It usually covers:

- Shopfront materials and glazing lines.
- Signage size and lighting.
- Approved working hours and noise.
- Contractor induction, insurance and safety documents.
- Waste removal and loading dock bookings.

Ask for the manual at lease signing. Designing joinery to the manual first is far cheaper than changing it after a centre review.

## Shop fitout timeline

A sensible shop programme runs joinery manufacture in parallel with site works.

1. Design concept and joinery list agreed.
2. Landlord or centre approval.
3. Site measure and shop drawings.
4. Joinery manufacture in the workshop while builders do partitions, electrical and flooring.
5. Joinery install, often after hours.
6. Final clean, stock load and opening.

Food premises add council and food-safety requirements, which usually extend the programme.

## Common shop fitout mistakes

New retailers in Newcastle often lose time and money in the same places.

- **Ordering joinery before landlord approval.** If the centre asks for changes, the counter may need to be rebuilt.
- **Forgetting the POS layout.** Card readers, scanners and cash drawers need power and data in the right place.
- **Under-sizing the stockroom.** Shelving depth should suit your real carton sizes.
- **Choosing soft finishes for heavy-use counters.** Laminate or compact laminate lasts far longer than painted MDF at the till.
- **No contingency.** Older buildings in the CBD and Darby Street often hide uneven floors and old services.
- **Leaving signage to the end.** Signage often needs separate approval and long lead times.

Planning these early keeps your opening date safe.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a shop fitout cost in Newcastle?

A retail shop fitout commonly sits between about $500 and $2,000 per square metre, with cafes and food shops higher because of services and kitchens. These are indicative 2026 planning ranges, not a quote. The tenancy condition, landlord requirements and amount of custom joinery decide the final figure.

### How long does a shop fitout take?

A small retail fitout with existing services can be built in a few weeks once design and landlord approval are complete. Joinery is manufactured off site in parallel, so the shop is closed for the shortest possible time. Food premises and centre tenancies usually take longer because of approvals.

### Do shopping centres in Newcastle have fitout rules?

Most shopping centres issue a tenancy design manual covering shopfront, materials, signage, working hours and contractor induction. Your design must usually be approved by the centre before work starts. Ask for the manual at lease signing so the joinery is designed to it.

### What joinery does a typical shop need?

Most shops need a service counter, wall display shelving, a feature or promotional display, stockroom shelving and sometimes fitting rooms. Cafes add a coffee counter and back bar. Each item should be priced separately so you can compare quotes properly.

### Can a shop fitout be done after hours?

Yes. Joinery can be built in the workshop and installed at night or over a weekend to protect trading. After-hours work costs more, so tell the shopfitter early if your lease or centre requires it.
