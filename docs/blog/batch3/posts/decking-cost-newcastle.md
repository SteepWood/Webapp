# Decking Cost Newcastle 2026: Timber vs Composite per m²

Summer in Newcastle is lived outside, and a good deck is the room that gets used most from November to March. The question is what it costs. This guide sets out decking cost Newcastle homeowners are being quoted in 2026, how timber compares with composite, and the approval and coastal issues that change the price.

We are carpenters and joiners based in Newcastle. Decks are carpentry work, and we build them with the same care we give our joinery.

## Deck cost per square metre in Newcastle

Deck pricing is usually quoted per square metre of finished deck, including the subframe, boards, fixings and labour.

| Deck type | Indicative Newcastle and Hunter range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timber deck | [$255–$505 per m²](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/deck-building-and-construction-nsw) | Treated pine at the low end, hardwood higher |
| Composite deck | [$355–$705 per m²](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/deck-building-and-construction-nsw) | Capped boards and hidden clips cost more |
| Balustrade | [$125–$405 per lineal metre](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/deck-building-and-construction-nsw) | Timber, glass or stainless wire |
| Elevated or multi-level | [$1,100–$1,500+ per m²](https://deckingnewcastle.com.au/custom-deck-design-build/) | Engineering, posts and stairs drive cost |

Across Australia, a medium [30 square metre deck commonly costs $6,000–$12,000 in timber or $7,500–$13,500 in composite](https://www.britedecking.com.au/how-much-does-a-deck-cost/). Small decks cost more per square metre because setup costs are spread over fewer boards.

These are indicative 2026 ranges for planning only. A site measure is needed for a real price.

## Timber vs composite decking

The board choice drives both upfront cost and long-term work.

| Factor | Hardwood timber | Composite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | Natural grain, colour varies | Consistent colour, timber-look patterns |
| Upfront cost | Usually lower than premium composite | Usually higher |
| Maintenance | Clean and re-oil, commonly [$15–$35 per m²](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/deck-staining-and-oiling-nsw) per treatment | Wash only |
| Heat | Stays cooler in light species | Dark boards get hot in full sun |
| Repair | Can be sanded and re-oiled | Scratches cannot be sanded out |
| Bushfire | Some species rated to BAL-29 | Only products with test evidence |

For a west-facing deck in Merewether or Belmont that cops afternoon sun, the oiling cycle on timber is real work. For a shaded deck in Lambton, hardwood can look beautiful for decades with less effort.

## Best timbers for Newcastle decks

Hardwood is still the most popular choice in the Hunter. WoodSolutions lists [Blackbutt, Spotted Gum and Merbau as bushfire-resisting timbers for decking up to BAL-29](https://www.woodsolutions.com.au/publications/domestic-timber-deck-design), each with an above-ground durability class 1 rating. Our [timber species guide](/blog/joinery-timber-species-guide-nsw) covers these species for indoor joinery as well.

- **Spotted Gum** — hard, durable, light to dark brown, good for exposed decks.
- **Blackbutt** — pale and even, popular for a modern coastal look.
- **Merbau** — rich red-brown, stable, but bleeds tannin in the first months.
- **Treated pine** — budget option for sheltered or low-use decks.

## Approvals: when a deck is exempt

Many backyard decks do not need council approval. Under NSW planning rules, a deck can be exempt development if it is [no more than 25 square metres, has a floor no more than 1 metre above existing ground and is set back at least 900 mm from the boundary in most zones](https://planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/development-assessment/planning-approval-pathways/development-types/balconies-decks-and-patios). If it is on bushfire-prone land within 5 metres of the house, non-combustible materials are required for exempt work.

Bigger, higher or heritage-area decks need a complying development certificate or development application. We check this at the measure so the design suits the approval path.

### Mine subsidence districts

Large parts of Newcastle and Lake Macquarie sit inside declared [Mine Subsidence Districts](https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/subsidence-advisory/mine-subsidence-districts). Subsidence Advisory NSW notes that [small decks are among the minor works that do not need its approval](https://www.nsw.gov.au/subsidence-advisory/development), but larger or elevated decks that need council approval may also need Subsidence Advisory sign-off. You can check your property on the NSW Planning Portal Spatial Viewer before you design the deck.

## What a fair deck quote should show

A deck quote should be easy to compare line by line. Before you sign, check that it lists:

- Total deck area in square metres and the board species or composite brand.
- Board width, thickness and fixing method (face screws or hidden clips).
- Subframe material, joist spacing and footing type.
- Stairs, landings and balustrade, priced separately.
- Fixings grade, especially stainless steel near the coast.
- Who handles approvals, certification and any engineering.
- Site clean-up and waste removal.
- Oil or coating, and whether the first coat is included.

If two quotes differ by thousands, the difference is usually in the subframe, the fixings or the balustrade, not the boards.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a deck cost per square metre in Newcastle?

Newcastle and Hunter timber decks are commonly quoted around $255 to $505 per square metre built, and composite decks around $355 to $705 per square metre. Elevated decks, stairs and balustrades add to that. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### Is composite decking worth it in Newcastle?

Composite costs more upfront but removes the regular oiling that timber needs, which commonly costs $15 to $35 per square metre each time in NSW. On a sunny, exposed deck the maintenance saving can close the gap over the years. Dark composite boards can get hot underfoot, so choose a lighter colour for west-facing decks.

### Do I need council approval for a deck in Newcastle?

A deck can be exempt development in NSW if it meets the Codes SEPP standards, including no more than 25 square metres, a floor no more than 1 metre above ground and at least 900 mm from the boundary in most zones. Larger or higher decks need a complying development certificate or development application. Bushfire-prone and heritage land have extra rules.

### What is the best timber for decking in Newcastle?

Spotted Gum, Blackbutt and Merbau are the most common choices because they are durable above ground and are listed as bushfire-resisting timbers for decks up to BAL-29. Treated pine is cheaper and suits budget or sheltered decks. Your choice should suit sun exposure, bushfire rating and budget.

### How long does it take to build a deck?

A simple ground-level deck of around 20 square metres is often built in about a week once materials are on site. Elevated decks, stairs, balustrades and approvals extend the timeline. Wet weather can also delay footing and framing work.
