# How Long Does an Office Fitout Take? NSW Timeline Guide

The first question most businesses ask about a new office is cost. The second, and often more important, is time. How long does an office fitout take, and when do you need to start so staff can move in on the right day? This guide breaks down a realistic NSW office fitout timeline stage by stage, with the joinery lead times that often catch people out.

SteepWood manufactures office joinery in Newcastle for fitouts across Newcastle, the Central Coast and Sydney, so we see programs from the joinery side every week.

## The short answer

| Office size | On-site construction | Design and approvals before site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small (under about 300 m²) | [1–2 weeks](https://www.progressivecorporate.com.au/fitout-hub/office-fitouts/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/) | [6–10 weeks](https://www.progressivecorporate.com.au/fitout-hub/office-fitouts/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/) |
| Medium | [3–6 weeks](https://www.progressivecorporate.com.au/fitout-hub/office-fitouts/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/) | 6–10 weeks |
| Large | [6–16 weeks](https://www.progressivecorporate.com.au/fitout-hub/office-fitouts/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/) | Often longer |

In Sydney, SB Projects estimates [4 to 12 weeks on site plus 3 to 6 weeks of design, or 7 to 18 weeks in total](https://sbprojects.com.au/2026/09/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/). Vestra notes that the [whole process from planning to move-in can run 6 to 12 months](https://vestra.com.au/insights/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take) when it starts with a lease search.

## Stage-by-stage timeline

| Stage | Typical duration | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief and budget | 1–3 weeks | Headcount, rooms, style, budget |
| Site search and lease | Varies | Inspect, negotiate, sign |
| Concept and design | 3–6 weeks | Layout, finishes, joinery concept |
| Landlord review | [2–4 weeks](https://completeofficefitouts.com.au/blogs/full-office-fit-out-timeframes-explained-from-concept-to-completion/) | Building manager approves design |
| Approvals and certification | [3–4 weeks](https://vestra.com.au/insights/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take) | Council or certifier where required |
| Tender and pricing | 2–3 weeks | Builder and joiner quotes |
| Shop drawings and manufacture | A few weeks | Joinery built off site |
| Construction | 1–16 weeks | Partitions, services, ceilings, flooring, install |
| Defects and commissioning | [1–2 weeks](https://completeofficefitouts.com.au/blogs/full-office-fit-out-timeframes-explained-from-concept-to-completion/) | Fix items, test services, clean |
| Move-in | Days | IT, furniture, staff |

Some stages overlap. The best programs run joinery manufacture at the same time as early site works.

## Where joinery fits in the program

Joinery is often on the critical path because it is custom. Reception desks, kitchenettes and storage walls cannot be bought off the shelf at the last minute. The sequence is:

1. Joinery design within the overall fitout design.
2. Site measure once partitions are up, or from accurate drawings.
3. Shop drawings for approval.
4. Material ordering, including special laminates, veneers and hardware.
5. Workshop manufacture.
6. Delivery and install near the end of construction.
7. Stone or solid surface tops templated and fitted.

## The most common causes of delay

- **Changing the design after approval.** Every change restarts parts of the process.
- **Slow landlord responses.** Submit complete documents to avoid back-and-forth.
- **Long-lead materials.** Special glass, imported finishes and some appliances.
- **Late IT and AV decisions.** Joinery needs cable and equipment positions.
- **Base building surprises.** Old services, asbestos or uneven slabs.
- **Holiday shutdowns.** Suppliers and trades close over Christmas.

## How to shorten your timeline

1. **Engage early.** Bring designers and the joiner in during lease negotiation.
2. **Make decisions once.** Lock in finishes, appliances and IT positions early.
3. **Choose available materials.** Standard laminate ranges are faster than special orders.
4. **Run manufacture in parallel.** Off-site joinery while partitions go up.
5. **Stage the work.** If you must stay open, zone the fitout.
6. **Keep a single decision-maker.** One person approving drawings saves days.

## A sample small-office program

For a 150 m² office with reception, kitchenette and storage, a realistic program from signed lease is often around three months: several weeks for design and landlord approval, a few weeks for shop drawings and manufacture alongside site works, a short construction period and a week for defects and move-in. Your own program will depend on the building, approvals and how quickly decisions are made.

## A checklist for the first month

If you have just decided to move or refit, these steps in the first four weeks set up the whole program.

- Confirm headcount now and in two to three years.
- List every room you need, including storage, print, kitchenette and quiet rooms.
- Set a budget range and a target move-in date.
- Ask the landlord for base building drawings and the fitout guide.
- Book a measure with your designer and joiner.
- Decide who signs off designs and variations.
- Talk to your IT provider about data, Wi-Fi and AV needs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does an office fitout take?

On-site construction often takes 1 to 2 weeks for a small office, 3 to 6 weeks for a medium office and 6 to 16 weeks for a large fitout. Design and approvals commonly add 6 to 10 weeks beforehand. Planning from lease decision to move-in can take several months.

### What causes office fitout delays?

Late design decisions, slow landlord approval, long-lead items such as special glass, stone or equipment, and changes after joinery is ordered are the most common causes. Base building issues found during demolition also add time.

### How long does office joinery take to make?

Once shop drawings are approved and materials confirmed, office joinery is typically manufactured in a few weeks. Special laminates, veneers or stone can extend this, so check lead times at the design stage.

### Can an office fitout happen while we keep working?

Yes, with staging. Work can be done in zones, after hours or over weekends. It takes longer and can cost more, but it avoids a full shutdown.

### When should I start planning an office relocation?

Start well before your lease expires. Some guides suggest the full relocation process, from search to move-in, can run 6 to 12 months. Even a small fitout benefits from at least three months of planning.
