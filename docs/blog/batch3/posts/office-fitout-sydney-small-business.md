# Small Office Fitout Sydney: Costs and Joinery for 2026

A small office fitout in Sydney is a big investment for a growing business. Rents are high, building rules are strict and every square metre has to work. The good news is that small offices do not need a corporate-scale budget to look professional. Most of the impression comes from a few well-built joinery pieces: reception, kitchenette and storage.

SteepWood builds office joinery in our Newcastle workshop and installs across Sydney, from the CBD and North Sydney to Parramatta, the Inner West and the Northern Beaches. This guide covers costs, joinery packages, timelines and practical ways to stretch your budget.

## Sydney building rules to plan for

Sydney office buildings often have strict rules:

- **Building manager approval** for drawings and contractors.
- **After-hours work** for noisy or messy tasks in occupied buildings.
- **Loading dock and lift bookings** for joinery delivery.
- **Insurance certificates** before access is granted.
- **Fire and services** checks if ceilings, sprinklers or exits are affected.
- **Strata rules** in small commercial strata buildings.

Workshop-built joinery with pre-finished panels reduces noise and dust on site, which building managers appreciate.

## Before you sign the lease

Many fitout costs are decided at lease stage. Before signing, check:

- **Make-good clause:** What must be removed or restored when you leave. Loose or modular joinery can reduce make-good costs.
- **Landlord contribution:** Some landlords offer a fitout incentive or rent-free period, which can fund joinery.
- **Existing fitout:** A space with usable partitions, kitchenette and services saves tens of thousands of dollars.
- **Building rules:** Ask for the fitout guide, approved contractor list and work-hour restrictions.
- **Services capacity:** Confirm power, data and air conditioning suit your team size.

A short site visit with your joiner before signing can flag joinery that is reusable and items that will be hard to install.

## Ways to stretch a small budget

1. **Reuse what works:** Existing partitions, ceilings and lighting can often stay.
2. **Keep plumbing where it is:** Moving the kitchenette is expensive.
3. **Spend where people look:** Reception and meeting rooms get premium finishes; back-of-house gets durable laminate.
4. **Standardise storage:** Repeating modules costs less than one-off pieces.
5. **Plan once:** Changes after manufacture are the most costly kind.

## Common small office mistakes

- Reception counter too big for the space.
- Kitchenette with no dishwasher or bin space.
- Not enough storage, leading to clutter on desks.
- Finishes chosen for looks, not durability.
- Joinery ordered before the building manager approves drawings.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a small office fitout cost in Sydney?

Sydney office fitouts are commonly estimated at around $1,800 to $3,500 or more per square metre, with JLL putting the Sydney average near $2,963 per square metre. Small, simple refreshes can cost much less. These are indicative 2026 figures, not a quote.

### How long does a small office fitout take?

Construction for a small office is often one to two weeks once design and approvals are complete. Design, building approvals and joinery manufacture usually add several weeks before work starts.

### What joinery does a small office need?

Most small offices need a reception counter, a kitchenette, storage and filing, a print and utility area, and sometimes meeting room joinery or a feature wall. Joinery is often the most visible part of the fitout.

### Can joinery be made outside Sydney?

Yes. SteepWood builds joinery in our Newcastle workshop and installs in Sydney. Workshop manufacture keeps site time short, which helps in buildings with limited access hours.

### How can a small business reduce fitout costs?

Reuse existing partitions and services, keep wet areas where plumbing already exists, focus custom joinery on reception and kitchen, and choose durable laminate over premium finishes in back-of-house areas.
