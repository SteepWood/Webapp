## Why small tenancies cost more per metre

A 40 square metre boutique in Paddington still needs:

- A full shopfront and signage.
- A service counter with POS and storage.
- Wall display along most walls.
- Lighting design and a switchboard.
- Fitting rooms or a stockroom.
- Design fees and centre or landlord approval.

These items do not shrink much with floor area, so the cost per square metre rises sharply.

## Centre and landlord requirements

Large Sydney centres usually have detailed tenancy design manuals and fitout coordinators. Expect:

- Design submission and approval before construction.
- Approved contractor lists or strict induction and insurance checks.
- Night or early-morning working hours.
- Fire and material requirements for shopfronts and bulkheads.
- Dock bookings and waste rules.

High street shops in Newtown, Mosman or Manly have simpler landlord approvals but tighter access: no loading dock, clearways and limited parking. We plan deliveries for early mornings and keep vehicle time short.

## Program: protecting opening day

1. Lease signed and design manual received.
2. Design and joinery concept.
3. Centre or landlord approval.
4. Shop drawings and material orders.
5. Joinery manufacture while builders do site works.
6. Install over a few nights.
7. Defects, clean and stock-in.

Rent-free periods in Sydney are valuable. Running manufacture in parallel with site works is the best way to use them.

## Budget tips

- **Use standard laminate colours.** Faster supply, lower cost.
- **Choose modular display systems** that can grow with the business.
- **Spend on lighting.** It lifts products more than expensive materials.
- **Keep the counter compact.** Space on the floor is space to sell.
- **Ask about a landlord contribution.** It is common in centre deals.
- **Get the cafe or food component priced separately** if you sell food. Our [cafe fitout cost guide](/blog/cafe-fitout-cost-nsw) explains why.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a retail shop fitout cost in Sydney?

Sydney retail fitouts for local brands are commonly quoted around $2,000 to $4,500 per square metre, and small tenancies under 50 square metres can cost up to twice the per-metre rate. Simpler fitouts can come in lower. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### Why are small shop fitouts so expensive per square metre?

Fixed costs such as design, approvals, shopfront, signage, electrical boards and the service counter are spread over fewer square metres. A 40 square metre boutique still needs most of the same items as a 120 square metre store.

### Do you build joinery in Newcastle for Sydney shops?

Yes. Joinery is manufactured in our Newcastle workshop, then delivered and installed in Sydney. Workshop manufacture keeps on-site time short, which helps with centre night-work rules and rent-free deadlines.

### What is a tenancy design manual?

It is the shopping centre's rule book for tenant fitouts, covering shopfront design, materials, signage, lighting, working hours and approvals. Your design usually needs centre approval before work starts.

### How can I reduce my Sydney retail fitout cost?

Reuse existing services where possible, choose durable standard laminates, use modular or mobile displays, keep the shopfront simple and lock in decisions early. Negotiating a landlord contribution can also help.
