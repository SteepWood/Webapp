# Home Office Built Ins for Hybrid Work: NSW Design Guide

Home office built ins have moved from a nice extra to an everyday need. With hybrid work now normal for many NSW households, a desk squeezed into the corner of the dining room no longer works three days a week. Good built-in joinery gives you a proper desk, storage for work gear, a tidy video-call backdrop and the ability to put work away at the end of the day.

SteepWood designs and builds home office joinery in our Newcastle workshop and installs across Newcastle, the Central Coast and Sydney. This guide covers layouts, details, costs and room-by-room ideas.

## What hybrid work needs from a home office

| Need | Joinery answer |
| --- | --- |
| Comfortable desk | Correct height and depth, room for legs and chair |
| Two screens or a laptop and screen | Desk at least 1,500 mm wide where possible |
| Paperwork and devices | Closed cupboards and file drawers |
| Video calls | Tidy shelving or panelling behind you |
| Cables and chargers | Grommets, cable trays, power at desk height |
| Switching off | Doors or a closable nook |
| Shared use | Two desk positions or adjustable storage |

## Desk sizing basics

Getting the desk right is the most important part. Ergonomic guides suggest a [desk depth that keeps the screen at a comfortable distance](https://ergoexperts.com.au/what-is-the-correct-desk-size/), and fixed desk height should suit the main user. The [Desky height calculator](https://desky.com.au/pages/desk-height-calculator) is a useful starting point.

| Dimension | Common range |
| --- | --- |
| Desk height | Around 720–750 mm (fixed) |
| Desk depth | 600–750 mm |
| Single-user width | 1,200–1,800 mm |
| Two-user width | 2,400 mm or more |
| Knee clearance | Keep the area under the desk open |

If more than one person uses the desk, consider a sit-stand frame within the joinery.

## Five layouts that work

1. **Full wall:** Desk across the wall with overhead shelves and base cupboards. Best for a dedicated study.
2. **Desk nook:** Desk in an alcove or built into a wardrobe space, with doors to close it away. See our [built-in desk and study nook guide](/blog/built-in-desk-study-nook-sydney).
3. **L-shape:** Desk on two walls for more surface and storage.
4. **Window desk:** Desk under a window with towers either side, good for natural light.
5. **Guest room combo:** Desk plus wall bed or storage so the room works as a guest room.

## The video-call backdrop

What colleagues see behind you matters more in hybrid work. Built-in joinery gives you control:

- Open shelves with a few books and objects, not clutter.
- Closed cupboards to hide files and equipment.
- Timber battens or a painted panel for a calm background.
- LED strip lighting on shelves for soft light.

Avoid putting a window directly behind your chair, as it makes your face dark on camera.

## Cable management and power

Plan these before joinery is built:

- Power points above the desk for laptop and lamp.
- A cable tray or channel under the desk.
- Grommets in the desktop for cables.
- A ventilated cupboard for the modem or printer.
- Data or USB-C outlets where needed.

Cable management keeps the desk clear and makes cleaning easier.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do home office built ins cost in NSW?

A built-in desk with some storage commonly starts around $3,000 to $8,000, while a full wall of desk, shelving and cabinetry can run $8,000 to $18,000 or more. Size, finishes and hardware drive the price. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### What should a hybrid work home office include?

A desk at the right height, closed storage for paperwork and devices, shelving for a tidy video-call backdrop, good cable management and power, and somewhere to put work away at the end of the day.

### What is the right desk height for a built-in desk?

A common fixed desk height is around 720 to 750 mm, but the right height depends on the user and chair. Many people add a sit-stand desk within the joinery for flexibility.

### Can home office joinery close away after work?

Yes. Desk nooks with pocket doors, bi-fold doors or a fold-down desk let you close the workspace at the end of the day, which suits living rooms and guest bedrooms.

### Do built ins add value to a home?

Well-made built-in joinery is generally seen as a plus by buyers, especially a functional study area in family homes. The value depends on design quality and whether it suits the room.
