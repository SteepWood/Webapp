# Cafe Fitout Cost NSW 2026: Per m² Prices and Joinery Budget

Opening a cafe is one of the most exciting and expensive fitouts a small business can take on. The coffee machine is the star, but the counter, kitchen, services and approvals are where the money goes. This guide sets out cafe fitout cost figures for NSW in 2026, with worked budgets, the joinery that matters most and practical ways to keep the total under control.

Small tenancies cost more per square metre. Liteco notes that [tenancies under 50 square metres can cost up to twice the per-metre rate](https://liteco.com.au/cost-to-open-cafe-restaurant-sydney/) because fixed costs such as exhaust and plumbing are spread over less space.

The biggest swing factor is whether you need a full commercial kitchen with exhaust or just a coffee and cold-food setup.

## Where the money goes

A typical cafe budget splits into these groups:

- **Services:** plumbing, grease trap, power upgrades, exhaust and air conditioning.
- **Kitchen:** commercial equipment, stainless benches, coolroom or fridges.
- **Joinery:** coffee counter, back bar, display, seating, waiter station.
- **Finishes:** flooring, wall linings, lighting, signage.
- **Fees:** design, council approval, certification, engineering.
- **Contingency:** usually 10 to 15 per cent.

Joinery is not the largest line, but it is the most visible. It shapes the customer experience and staff workflow every day.

## Food premises rules

Cafes in NSW must meet the food premises fitout standard and council requirements. In practice, this means:

- Smooth, impervious, cleanable surfaces in food areas.
- Hand-wash basins in the right places.
- Sealed joinery with no gaps where pests or food can collect.
- Adequate lighting and ventilation.
- Council approval before trading.

Our [shopfitting cost by format](/blog/shopfitting-cost-by-format-nsw) guide links to council food premises guidance and covers the approval steps in more detail. For joinery, it means choosing laminates, compact laminate and sealed edges rather than raw timber in food prep zones.

## Ways to save on a cafe fitout

1. **Take over an existing food premises.** Grease traps, exhaust and plumbing may already be in place.
2. **Keep the kitchen simple.** A smaller menu can mean smaller equipment and less exhaust.
3. **Refurbish the counter rather than replace it.** Refacing can be much cheaper.
4. **Spend on what customers touch.** Counter, seating and lighting matter more than back-of-house finishes.
5. **Lock in the equipment list early.** Late changes to machines and fridges cause joinery rework.
6. **Plan the program.** Every week of delay before opening costs rent and wages.

## Timeline

Cafe fitouts usually take longer than retail because of services and approvals. Design and council approval can take weeks, and site works must be coordinated with plumbers, electricians and the exhaust contractor. Joinery is manufactured off site while services go in, then installed near the end so it stays clean and undamaged.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a cafe fitout cost in NSW?

Cafe fitouts in Australia are commonly quoted around $1,500 to $4,500 per square metre, depending on the kitchen, services and finishes. A mid-range cafe of 80 to 150 square metres often lands around $150,000 to $350,000. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### Why do cafe fitouts cost more than retail?

Cafes need commercial kitchens, exhaust, grease traps, extra plumbing and power, food-safe finishes and council approvals. Those services and compliance items add significantly to the cost per square metre compared with a dry retail shop.

### How much does a cafe coffee counter cost?

A custom coffee counter is priced on length, finish, tops and built-in equipment such as knock boxes, bin chutes and under-counter fridges. It is often one of the largest joinery items in a cafe. Ask for the counter and back bar to be priced separately.

### Can I save money with a second-hand cafe fitout?

Taking over an existing cafe can save a lot on services and kitchen, but check that the equipment works, the exhaust complies and the joinery is in good condition. Refacing an existing counter is often cheaper than replacing it.

### Do I need council approval for a cafe fitout?

Most new food premises need council approval and must meet the food premises fitout standard. Requirements vary by council, so check with your local council, such as City of Newcastle or Central Coast Council, before finalising the design.
