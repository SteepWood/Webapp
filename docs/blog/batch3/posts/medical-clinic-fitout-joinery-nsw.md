# Medical Fitout Newcastle and NSW: Clinic Joinery Guide 2026

A medical clinic has to work for patients, practitioners and cleaners at the same time. The reception must feel welcoming, consult rooms must be practical and every surface in a treatment room must be easy to clean. This guide covers medical fitout Newcastle and NSW practices are planning in 2026: costs per square metre, the joinery a clinic needs, surface choices and realistic timelines.

## Choosing clinic surfaces

Infection control starts with surfaces that can be cleaned properly.

- **High-pressure laminate** is durable, smooth and affordable for most joinery.
- **Compact laminate** is solid, water-resistant and suits treatment benches and wet areas.
- **Solid surface** allows seamless tops with integrated sinks.
- **Sealed edges and joints** stop moisture and contamination getting into board.
- **Easy-clean handles** or finger pulls avoid hard-to-clean hardware.

Your infection control adviser and designer should confirm the final specification. We build to that specification and advise on what works in practice.

## Compliance and classification

Medical fitouts often involve extra compliance. Sydney Fitout Group notes that many medical tenancies fall under [NCC Class 9a requirements](https://sydneyfitoutgroup.com.au/medical-fitouts-sydney-costs-compliance-process/), which affects fire safety, access and services. Not every clinic is Class 9a; a building certifier should confirm the classification for your tenancy and use.

Accessibility matters too. Reception counters, consult rooms and bathrooms should be designed so patients with disabilities can use them comfortably.

## Medical fitout timeline

Sydney Fitout Group estimates medical fitouts commonly take [10 to 20 weeks](https://sydneyfitoutgroup.com.au/medical-fitouts-sydney-costs-compliance-process/) from design to completion. Our [office fitout timeline guide](/blog/office-fitout-timeline-nsw) covers the general stages. For clinics, add time for:

- Plumbing to multiple consult and treatment rooms.
- Equipment coordination, especially dental.
- Certification and any accreditation inspections.
- Commissioning of services.

Joinery is manufactured off site during construction, then installed once walls, services and flooring are complete.

## Planning tips for practice owners

1. Confirm the number of consult and treatment rooms before design starts.
2. Lock in equipment models early so joinery fits around them.
3. Plan storage for the practice in two to three years, not just today.
4. Separate clean and dirty workflows.
5. Allow budget for accessible design at reception and in bathrooms.
6. Ask for joinery shop drawings before manufacture.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a medical fitout cost in Newcastle?

GP clinic fitouts are commonly quoted around $1,400 to $2,200 per square metre in a warm shell, with dental around $1,200 to $1,800 per square metre before specialist equipment. Wider Australian ranges of $1,500 to $3,000 per square metre are also common. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### What surfaces are best for clinic joinery?

Smooth, non-porous, easy-to-clean surfaces such as high-pressure laminate, compact laminate and solid surface are common in clinics. Joints and edges should be sealed so they can be wiped down and disinfected. Your infection control adviser should confirm requirements.

### How long does a medical clinic fitout take?

Medical fitouts commonly take longer than offices because of services, compliance and equipment. Sydney guides suggest around 10 to 20 weeks for a medical fitout program. Joinery is manufactured off site to save time.

### Do medical fitouts need special building approvals?

Medical and health-care buildings can have different classifications under the National Construction Code, which affects fire, access and services requirements. A building certifier should confirm the classification and approvals for your tenancy.

### What joinery does a GP clinic need?

Most GP clinics need a reception desk, consult room desks and storage, treatment room benches with sinks, clean and dirty utility storage, staff kitchenette and waiting room joinery. Each item should be drawn and priced separately.
