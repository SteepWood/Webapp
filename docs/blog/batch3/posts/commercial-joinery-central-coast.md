## Local joiner, shopfitter or flat pack

Central Coast businesses usually choose between three options. A shopfitter or fitout builder manages the whole project, including partitions, ceilings, services and joinery. A specialist joiner builds and installs the joinery itself, either for the business owner or as a subcontractor to the builder. Flat-pack or stock commercial furniture is the cheapest option for simple storage, but it rarely fits the space exactly and often does not survive heavy commercial use.

For many small Coast tenancies, such as a medical suite in Gosford or a retail shop in Erina, the most cost-effective path is a local builder or the owner managing a few trades, with a specialist joiner supplying the counters, storage and kitchenette. That keeps the joinery quality high while avoiding a full head-contractor margin on simple works. For bigger tenancies, centre requirements and services coordination usually make a fitout builder the better lead, with joinery as a subcontract package.

## Choosing a commercial joiner

| Question to ask | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Is the joiner licensed? | Check with Service NSW |
| Do they have public liability? | Required by most landlords and builders |
| Do they provide shop drawings? | Avoids surprises on site |
| Who installs? | Own team or subcontractors |
| What warranty applies? | Structural and hardware warranties |
| Have they worked in centres? | Centre rules and after-hours work |

You can [check a builder or tradesperson licence](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/check-a-builder-or-tradesperson-licence) online.
