A deck can be the best value addition to a Sydney home, turning a sloping backyard into an outdoor room. It can also be one of the most frustrating projects if the builder is wrong for the job. This guide helps you compare deck builders Sydney homeowners are hiring in 2026: what decks cost by material, what drives the price, the approval rules and the questions to ask.

## What drives the price of a Sydney deck

| Cost driver | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Height above ground | Elevated decks need engineering, posts, bracing and balustrades |
| Slope of the block | More footings and longer posts |
| Access | Narrow side paths and steep driveways slow work |
| Board species or brand | Premium hardwoods and capped composite cost more |
| Board fixing | Hidden clips cost more than face screws |
| Stairs and landings | Each flight adds labour and materials |
| Approvals | Certifier, engineering and council fees |
| Demolition | Removing an old deck adds time and disposal |

On a typical Northern Beaches or North Shore sloping block, the subframe and footings can cost as much as the boards.

## Hardwood vs composite in Sydney

Our [composite vs timber comparison](/blog/composite-vs-timber-decking-central-coast) covers the details. In short:

- **Hardwood** looks natural, can be sanded and refreshed, and species like Spotted Gum and Blackbutt are listed as [bushfire-resisting for use up to BAL-29](https://www.woodsolutions.com.au/publications/domestic-timber-deck-design).
- **Composite** avoids oiling, which in NSW commonly costs [$15–$35 per m²](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/deck-staining-and-oiling-nsw) each time, but only some products carry bushfire test evidence.

## Approvals in Sydney

Many Sydney decks need approval because of height, size or heritage controls. Under NSW rules, a deck can be exempt if it is [no more than 25 square metres, no more than 1 metre above existing ground, and set back at least 900 mm from side and rear boundaries in most zones](https://planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/development-assessment/planning-approval-pathways/development-types/balconies-decks-and-patios). On bushfire-prone land within 5 metres of the house, exempt decks must use non-combustible materials.

Larger, higher or heritage-area decks usually need a complying development certificate or a development application. Waterfront and steep blocks often need engineering. We confirm the pathway at the measure.

## Questions to ask Sydney deck builders

1. What licence do you hold, and can I see the number?
2. Is the subframe treated pine, hardwood or steel, and what joist spacing?
3. What fixings will you use, and are they stainless near the coast?
4. Who handles approvals and certification?
5. Are stairs, balustrades and demolition included?
6. What warranty applies to workmanship?
7. When is payment due, and what is the deposit?

In NSW, [deck building is listed under carpentry work](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/carpentry-work) and also under [structural landscaping](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/licences-and-credentials/building-and-trade-licences-and-registrations/structural-landscaping-work), and residential work over $5,000 needs a licence. You can [check a licence with Service NSW](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/check-a-builder-or-tradesperson-licence). Spring and early summer are the busiest months for deck builders in Sydney. If you want a deck for Christmas entertaining, book the measure early, because approvals and material supply can take weeks. Autumn and winter builds are often easier to schedule.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a deck cost in Sydney?

Sydney deck prices are commonly quoted around $480 to $580 per square metre for treated pine, $650 to $900 for hardwood, $600 to $800 for composite and $850 to $1,200 for premium capped composite, built. Elevated decks and balustrades cost more. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### Why are decks more expensive in Sydney than regional NSW?

Labour rates, access, parking, sloping blocks and higher approval and engineering requirements all add cost in Sydney. Regional NSW timber decks are often quoted from about $250 per square metre at the low end.

### Does my Sydney deck need council approval?

A deck can be exempt development in NSW if it meets the Codes SEPP standards, including no more than 25 square metres, a floor no more than 1 metre above ground and a 900 mm side setback in most zones. Larger or higher decks need a complying development certificate or development application.

### What licence should a deck builder have in NSW?

Deck building is listed under carpentry work, and also under structural landscaping, in NSW licensing. Residential work over $5,000 needs a contractor licence. Check the builder's licence with Service NSW before signing.

### Is hardwood or composite better for Sydney decks?

Hardwood suits buyers who want natural timber and do not mind regular oiling. Composite suits exposed, coastal or low-maintenance decks. Bushfire ratings, sun exposure and budget usually decide.
