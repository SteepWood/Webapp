# Custom Shop Counters and Display Joinery: A 2026 Guide

The counter is where every sale ends, and the display wall is where most of them start. Custom shop counters and display joinery shape how customers move through your shop, how staff work and how your products look. This guide covers counter design, POS layouts, materials, shelving, lighting and costs for retailers and cafes across NSW.

SteepWood builds counters and display joinery in our Newcastle workshop for shops in Newcastle, the Central Coast and Sydney.

## What a good counter does

A counter is a workstation, a display, a storage unit and a brand statement at the same time.

| Function | Design detail |
| --- | --- |
| Serve customers | Clear sightlines from the door, space for two staff at peak |
| Take payment | POS terminal, card reader, scanner and receipt printer positions |
| Store | Bags, wrapping, stationery and returns under the counter |
| Display | Impulse products at the front or on top |
| Secure | Lockable cash drawer and storage |
| Welcome all customers | Lower accessible section for wheelchair users and signing |

## Counter sizes and layout

| Element | Practical guide |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Fit POS, wrapping area and display; often 1.8–3 m for small shops |
| Depth | Around 600–800 mm, depending on equipment |
| Height | Commonly around 900–1,100 mm, with a lower accessible section |
| Staff space behind | Room for two people to pass |
| Customer side | Toe kick or recess so customers can stand close |

These are practical starting points, not regulations. Your designer or certifier should confirm accessibility requirements for your building.

## POS and cable planning

Power and data positions should be agreed before the counter is drawn.

- Cable grommets for terminals and card readers.
- A ventilated cupboard for the router, POS hub or printer.
- Power points inside the counter, not on the floor.
- A cash drawer housing sized to your model.
- Space for a barcode scanner and receipt printer.

## Counter materials

| Material | Pros | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-pressure laminate | Durable, affordable, many colours | Edges need protection |
| Compact laminate | Very tough, water-resistant, thin profile | Specialist machining |
| Timber veneer | Warm, premium look | Needs protection from knocks |
| Solid timber | Boutique and cafe character | Moves with humidity, needs care |
| Natural stone or porcelain | Premium, hard-wearing tops | Fabrication lead time and weight |
| Timber battens or tiles (front) | Strong visual identity | Clean detailing needed |

For high-traffic counters, laminate or compact laminate tops with a feature front is a common, durable combination. Laminex describes its multipurpose compact laminate as [scratch, wear, stain, moisture and impact resistant](https://www.laminex.com.au/brands/laminex/product-ranges/compact-laminate), and compact panels suit [exposed, machined edges](https://remicadecor.com/hpl-vs-compact-laminate/) without edge tape.

## Display joinery

| Display type | Best for | Specification tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wall shelving | Most retail | Adjustable shelves, rated for product weight |
| Slatwall or pegboard | Accessories, tools, small items | Flexible hooks and shelves |
| Gondolas or islands | Browsing and promotion | Mobile bases for flexible layouts |
| Glass cabinets | High-value items | Lockable, internal lighting |
| Plinths and tables | Hero products and windows | Movable, durable tops |

Shelf loads matter. Books, wine, tins and ceramics are heavy, so shelves need the right thickness, span and supports. Tell your joiner what will sit on each shelf.

## Lighting with joinery

Integrated lighting lifts a display more than expensive materials. LED strips under shelves, in glass cabinets and along counter fronts highlight products and help the shop feel finished. Plan drivers and switching with your electrician before joinery is manufactured.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a custom shop counter cost?

A simple stock reception or service counter can start from around $1,198 plus GST, while custom shop counters are priced on length, materials, tops and built-in features. Large feature counters with stone tops and integrated displays cost considerably more. Ask for an itemised price.

### How high should a shop counter be?

Many service counters sit around standard bench height, roughly 900 to 1,100 mm depending on the task, with a lower section for accessibility and for customers to sign or rest bags. Your designer should confirm heights for your use and building requirements.

### What is the best material for a shop counter top?

High-pressure laminate is durable and affordable. Compact laminate is very tough and water-resistant. Natural stone and porcelain give a premium look, and timber suits boutique and cafe styles but needs more care.

### Can display shelving be adjustable?

Yes. Slotted channels, recessed shelf supports or adjustable systems let you change shelf heights as your stock changes. Specify shelf loads early, because heavy products need thicker shelves and stronger supports.

### How long does it take to make a custom shop counter?

After drawings are approved and materials confirmed, a counter is typically made in the workshop within a few weeks and installed in a day or less. Stone tops add fabrication time.
