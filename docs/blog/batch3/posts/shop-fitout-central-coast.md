## Shopping centre and landlord rules

Erina Fair, Westfield Tuggerah and other centres usually issue a tenancy design manual. Expect rules on:

- Shopfront design and bulkhead signage.
- Approved materials and fire ratings.
- Working hours, often nights only.
- Inductions, insurance certificates and safety documents.
- Waste removal and dock bookings.

Strip shops and village retail in Terrigal, Ettalong or The Entrance usually have simpler landlord approvals, but older buildings bring uneven floors and limited access.

## Timeline from lease to opening

1. Lease signed and design manual received.
2. Concept design and joinery list.
3. Landlord or centre approval.
4. Site measure and shop drawings.
5. Joinery manufacture in the workshop while builders handle partitions, electrical and flooring.
6. Install, often after hours.
7. Clean, stock and open.

Running workshop manufacture in parallel with site works is the biggest time saver in any shopfit.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Ordering joinery before centre approval.
- Leaving power and data positions undecided.
- Painted finishes on heavy-wear counters.
- No allowance for after-hours work in the budget.
- Under-sized stockrooms.

## Making a small shop work harder

Many Central Coast tenancies are small, especially in coastal villages. Good joinery makes a small shop feel organised and easy to shop.

- **Use the full wall height.** Display up high, stock storage above in closed cupboards.
- **Keep the floor open.** One or two mobile displays are better than a crowded floor.
- **Combine counter and storage.** Drawers and cupboards under the counter save stockroom space.
- **Plan lighting with the joinery.** Shelf lighting lifts products and the whole shop.
- **Choose a hard-wearing palette.** Laminate and compact laminate stand up to busy summer trade.
- **Think about the window.** A movable plinth makes changing the display easy.

A clear layout also helps staff work faster in peak periods, from school holidays to Christmas.

## Planning around the Central Coast trading year

Timing matters on the Coast. Summer holidays, Easter and the school holiday weeks bring visitors to Terrigal, The Entrance and Ettalong, and many shops do a large share of their trade in those weeks. Plan the fitout so the shop is open and settled before the busy period, not halfway through it. In practice, that means locking in design and approvals in the cooler months, manufacturing joinery while the old shop keeps trading, and installing in a short closure window. If the fitout must happen during peak season, night and weekend installation keeps the doors open.
