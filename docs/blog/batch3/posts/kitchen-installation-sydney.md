Good kitchen installation Sydney homeowners can rely on plans for all of it before the first cabinet arrives. ## Apartments and strata

Most Sydney apartment kitchens are installed under strata rules. Before install day we confirm:

- Whether the owners corporation needs to approve the renovation.
- Lift booking times and lift protection.
- Loading dock or parking arrangements.
- Approved working hours and noise limits.
- Building manager contact and insurance certificates.
- Waste removal rules.

Moving plumbing or exhaust in an apartment often needs strata approval and can be restricted, so keep the sink and cooktop in place where possible. Our [apartment and terrace joinery guide](/blog/apartment-terrace-joinery-sydney) covers more strata tips.

## Choosing a Sydney kitchen installer

1. Ask who installs: their own team or subcontractors.
2. Ask whether installation is included in the price.
3. Confirm exclusions: plumbing, electrical, tiling, painting and appliances.
4. Check the licence. Residential work over $5,000 needs one in NSW, and you can [check a licence with Service NSW](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/check-a-builder-or-tradesperson-licence).
5. Ask about strata experience and insurance certificates.
6. Ask how defects are handled after handover.

## Preparing for install day

- Clear the kitchen and access route.
- Confirm lift and parking bookings with the building manager.
- Set up a temporary kitchen elsewhere in the home.
- Have appliance models confirmed so cut-outs match.
- Agree start times and noise limits with the installer.

## What happens after installation

Once the benchtop is fitted and the plumber and electrician have connected the sink, cooktop and appliances, we return for final adjustments. Doors and drawers are aligned, soft-close settings checked and any small marks touched up. In Sydney apartments, we also remove common-area protection and confirm with the building manager that the lift and loading dock are clear. You receive care instructions for your finishes and hardware, and a contact for any adjustments in the months after handover. Kitchens settle as the seasons change, so a quick follow-up adjustment is normal and part of the service.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does kitchen installation cost in Sydney?

Cabinet installation commonly costs around $1,800 to $4,500, and Airtasker data shows Sydney kitchen installation tasks ranging from about $127 to $1,850 with a median near $990 for smaller jobs. Full custom kitchens are priced per linear metre, with installation usually included. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### What does a custom kitchen cost per linear metre in Sydney?

Sydney kitchens are commonly quoted around $800 to $1,400 per linear metre in laminate, $1,400 to $2,200 in polyurethane and $1,800 to $3,000 in 2-pac. Benchtops and appliances are usually separate.

### Can you install a kitchen in a strata apartment?

Yes. We follow the building's by-laws, book lifts and loading docks, protect common areas and work within approved hours. Some strata schemes require approval for kitchen renovations, especially if plumbing moves, so check before you start.

### Do you travel from Newcastle to Sydney for kitchens?

Yes. Kitchens are built in our Newcastle workshop and installed in Sydney by our own team. Because cabinets arrive ready to fit, time on site is kept short.

### How long does a Sydney kitchen installation take?

A standard custom kitchen is usually installed over a few days, then the benchtop is templated and fitted later, followed by plumbing and electrical fit-off. Strata hours and access can extend the schedule.
