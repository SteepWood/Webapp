# Office Fitout Central Coast: Costs and Planning for Gosford

The Central Coast is changing. Gosford has a new university campus, more professional firms are setting up closer to where their staff live, and office rents are well below Sydney. This guide covers office fitout Central Coast costs in 2026, the joinery most small offices need, and how to plan a fitout in Gosford, Erina, Tuggerah or Wyong without losing months.

SteepWood builds office joinery in our Newcastle workshop and installs across the Central Coast. We work directly with business owners and with builders delivering fitouts.

## The Central Coast office market in 2026

Office space on the Coast is available and relatively affordable. Raine and Horne Commercial reports [Gosford office vacancy around 20% with rents of about $200 to $300 per square metre](https://imags.com.au/RH_Commercial_Insights_H1_2026/), and notes the [University of Newcastle Gosford campus opened in January 2026](https://imags.com.au/RH_Commercial_Insights_H1_2026/). MarketBuddy lists [33 office and 21 medical lease listings in Gosford with a median of about $293 per square metre a year](https://www.marketbuddy.com.au/suburb/gosford-nsw-2250).

For tenants, that means choice and room to negotiate. A landlord with vacant space may offer fitout contributions or rent-free periods, which can pay for a good part of your joinery.

## Planning steps that save time

1. **Measure before you sign.** A quick measure shows what fits and what the fitout may cost.
2. **Ask the landlord for base building details.** Know what is included: ceilings, lighting, air conditioning, kitchenette.
3. **Agree the layout.** Number of desks, meeting rooms, reception and storage.
4. **Get landlord approval** for the design before ordering joinery.
5. **Manufacture joinery while site works happen.** This is where workshop-built joinery saves weeks.
6. **Install and hand over**, then move in.

Fitouts commonly need [6 to 10 weeks of design and approvals before construction starts](https://www.progressivecorporate.com.au/fitout-hub/office-fitouts/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/), so start early.

## Coastal conditions and materials

Offices near the water in Terrigal, The Entrance and Woy Woy face more humidity than inland sites. Moisture-resistant board in kitchenettes, sealed edges and stainless or coated hardware all extend joinery life. Air-conditioned offices are less affected, but kitchenettes and bathrooms still need care.

## Common office fitout mistakes

- **Underestimating storage.** Most teams need more cupboard space than they plan.
- **Late decisions on power and data.** Joinery needs to know where cables come through.
- **No accessible reception section.** Plan a lower counter for wheelchair users and comfortable conversation.
- **Forgetting make-good.** Check the lease for what must be removed at the end of the term.

## Budgeting tips for small Central Coast offices

A small office does not need a corporate budget to look professional. Spend where clients and staff notice it most.

- **Put money into the reception.** It is the first thing every client sees.
- **Keep partitions simple.** Glass meeting rooms cost more than open plan with a single closed room.
- **Use laminate for hard-working joinery.** Kitchenettes and storage last longer with durable finishes.
- **Reuse what works.** Existing ceilings, lighting and carpet can often stay with a refresh.
- **Ask the landlord for help.** With higher vacancy, contributions or rent-free periods are often negotiable.
- **Hold a contingency.** Ten per cent is sensible for older Gosford buildings.

These choices often save more than shopping around for a cheaper builder.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an office fitout cost on the Central Coast?

Basic NSW office fitouts are commonly quoted around $800 to $1,200 per square metre and mid-range fitouts around $1,200 to $1,800 per square metre. Premium finishes, new services and heavy joinery push costs higher. These are indicative 2026 planning ranges, not a quote.

### Is office space on the Central Coast cheaper than Sydney?

Generally yes. Raine and Horne Commercial reports Gosford office rents around $200 to $300 per square metre, far below Sydney CBD levels. Lower rent can free up budget for a better fitout and joinery.

### Do you travel from Newcastle to the Central Coast?

Yes. SteepWood is based in Newcastle and regularly measures and installs across the Central Coast, from Wyong and Tuggerah to Gosford, Erina and Terrigal. Joinery is built in our workshop and delivered ready to install.

### What joinery does a small office usually need?

Most small offices need a reception desk, a kitchenette, storage cupboards, a utility or print area and sometimes a meeting room credenza. Each item should be shown on drawings and priced separately so quotes can be compared.

### When should I start planning an office fitout?

Start as soon as you are negotiating the lease. Design, landlord approval and joinery manufacture can take longer than the on-site build. Knowing the fitout scope early also helps you negotiate incentives with the landlord.
