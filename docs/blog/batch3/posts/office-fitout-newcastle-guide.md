# Office Fitout Newcastle: 2026 Costs, Timelines and Joinery

An office fitout in Newcastle costs less than the same job in the Sydney CBD, but it is not a cheap exercise, and the joinery is where many budgets drift. This guide sets out what an office fitout Newcastle businesses are paying for in 2026, how long it takes, and how to brief the reception, tea point and storage so the quote you get is the job you get.

We build commercial joinery in our Newcastle workshop and install it from Honeysuckle to Charlestown, so this is written from the bench, not the sales desk.

## Why Newcastle office fitouts are busy in 2026

The Newcastle office market is moving. Colliers forecasts the [Newcastle A-grade vacancy rate to tighten from 15.4% to below 9% by January 2028](https://www.commo.com.au/news/2026/07/26/colliers-newcastle-office-market-underpinned-strong-fundamentals-growth-outlook), and reports that 36% of A-grade deals since 2021 have involved occupiers upgrading into more modern space. Ray White recorded [annual net absorption of 4,836 square metres in Newcastle](https://www.raywhitecommercial.com/research/australian-office-markets-strongest-tenant-demand-since-pre-pandemic-peak), describing the city as an affordable alternative to Sydney.

In plain terms, businesses are moving into better floors, and every move needs a fitout. That keeps local fitout and joinery workshops booked, which matters for your programme.

These ranges are indicative 2026 planning figures. They are not a SteepWood quote.

## Cat A, Cat B and where joinery sits

Before you compare quotes, confirm what the landlord is already providing.

- **Cat A** is the base build: ceilings, lighting, air conditioning and services ready for a tenant.
- **Cat B** is your fitout: partitions, reception, meeting rooms, tea point, storage, signage and furniture.

Almost all joinery sits in Cat B. If one quote assumes the landlord supplies a kitchenette and another does not, the two numbers are not comparable. Ask every contractor to list the joinery items separately.

## How long does it take?

Fitout timelines are mostly decided before anyone picks up a drill. Progressive Corporate puts construction at [one to two weeks for small offices, three to six weeks for medium offices and six to sixteen weeks for large or complex projects](https://www.progressivecorporate.com.au/fitout-hub/office-fitouts/how-long-does-an-office-fitout-take/), with design and approvals adding six to ten weeks before construction.

For joinery, the critical path looks like this:

1. Floor plan and joinery elevations agreed.
2. Site measure once walls are framed or existing walls are confirmed.
3. Shop drawings issued and signed off.
4. Materials ordered, including stone and specialty finishes.
5. Manufacture in the workshop.
6. Install, usually after flooring and before final paint touch-ups.

Late design changes after shop drawings are signed are the most common cause of delay we see. Lock the joinery early.

## How to get comparable office fitout quotes

A simple brief prevents most disputes later.

- Supply a floor plan with the joinery items marked.
- State the finish level for each item: laminate, 2-pac, veneer or stone.
- Confirm who supplies appliances, sinks and taps.
- Confirm building rules for access, noise and waste.
- Ask for a programme that shows joinery manufacture and install dates.
- Ask for licence, insurance and warranty details in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an office fitout cost in Newcastle?

Newcastle office fitouts commonly sit around $1,000 to $1,500 per square metre for a basic fitout and $1,500 to $2,800 for mid-range work, with premium fitouts higher. These are indicative 2026 planning ranges, not a quote. Building condition, services and the amount of custom joinery decide the final number.

### How long does an office fitout take in Newcastle?

Construction on a small office can take one to two weeks and a medium office three to six weeks, while design and approvals often add six to ten weeks before work starts. Joinery manufacture runs in parallel once drawings are signed off. Building access rules in the CBD can also change the programme.

### What is the difference between Cat A and Cat B fitout?

Cat A is the landlord base finish that makes a tenancy services-ready, such as ceilings, lighting and basic air conditioning. Cat B is the tenant fitout, including partitions, reception, tea point, storage and branding. Most joinery sits inside the Cat B scope.

### Do I need approval for an office fitout in Newcastle?

Many internal fitouts need landlord consent and some need a complying development certificate or development application, depending on the works and the building. Your fitout manager or certifier should confirm the pathway before construction is booked. Joinery itself rarely triggers approval, but services and partitions can.

### Can SteepWood build only the joinery for my office fitout?

Yes. SteepWood can supply and install the joinery package, such as reception counters, tea points, storage walls and credenzas, for a builder or project manager running the wider fitout. We can also coordinate a smaller fitout directly where the scope suits.
