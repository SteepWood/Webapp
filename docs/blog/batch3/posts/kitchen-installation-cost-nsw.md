# Kitchen Installation Cost NSW: What You Pay For in 2026

Kitchen installation cost in NSW is the part of the budget most people underestimate. The cabinets are priced in a showroom, but the work of getting them level, square, scribed and connected happens in your house, on your floors and against your walls. This guide explains what kitchen installation costs in 2026, what a fair quote includes, and where the extra charges come from.

If you want the full cost of a new kitchen, read our [full custom kitchen cost guide](/blog/custom-kitchen-cost-nsw-2026). This post focuses on the installation line.

## What a cabinet installation actually involves

Kitchen installation is not just screwing boxes to a wall. On a typical SteepWood install the sequence is:

1. Set a level datum line around the room, because floors in older Newcastle and Central Coast homes are rarely flat.
2. Fit adjustable legs or a levelled plinth under the base cabinets.
3. Fix base cabinets to the wall and to each other so runs are straight.
4. Hang overhead cabinets to a laser line.
5. Scribe end panels and fillers to walls that are out of plumb.
6. Fit tall pantry and appliance towers, checking oven and fridge clearances.
7. Adjust doors and drawers, fit kickboards and handles.
8. Prepare for benchtop templating.

Each step takes time in a real house. That is why a flat pack kitchen can cost more to install than people expect.

## Flat pack vs custom: where installation cost hides

A flat pack kitchen looks cheap on the box, but the labour does not disappear. The assembly, levelling, scribing and joining are the same skills a custom install needs, and a flat pack installer is doing them on the job site rather than in a workshop. Our [flat pack versus custom comparison](/blog/flat-pack-vs-custom-kitchen-australia) covers this in more depth.

| Item | Flat pack | Custom (supply and install) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cabinet assembly | On site, by installer or owner | In the workshop, before delivery |
| Fit to room | Fillers and site cuts | Built to measured sizes |
| Install quote | Separate, often hourly | Usually included in the joinery price |
| Warranty on install | Depends on installer | One business responsible for product and install |
| Typical cost per linear metre | [$180–$450 for flat pack supplied and installed](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/kitchen-renovation-cost-nsw) | [$650–$1,400 for custom cabinetry in quality board](https://www.thequoteyard.com.au/services/kitchen-renovation-cost-nsw) |

Custom cabinets cost more, but one business owns the result from measure to handover. For many households that single point of responsibility is worth the difference.

## The extras that change the total

Most disputes about kitchen installation come from items nobody listed. A typical cabinet maker quote covers cabinets, doors, hardware, scribing and kickboards, but [usually not electrical, plumbing, tiling, painting, rangehood ducting or appliance installation](https://kitchenquote.com.au/costs/kitchen-installation-cost/).

Watch for these:

- **Moving the sink or dishwasher.** Rerouting pipes typically costs [$800–$2,500](https://kitchenquote.com.au/costs/kitchen-installation-cost/).
- **Rangehood ducting.** Ducting through a wall or ceiling adds [$300–$800](https://kitchenquote.com.au/costs/kitchen-installation-cost/).
- **Switchboard upgrade.** Some older homes need one before new circuits, at [$1,500–$3,000](https://kitchenquote.com.au/costs/kitchen-installation-cost/).
- **Benchtop templating and fitting.** Stone, porcelain and sintered tops are measured after the cabinets are fixed.
- **Contingency.** Allow [10 to 15 per cent](https://buildpilot.com.au/learn/kitchens) for issues found behind the old kitchen.

## Licences, trades and who is responsible

In NSW, residential building work valued over [$5,000 including GST](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/running-a-business/advertising-laws-and-your-business/building-trade-advertisements) needs a contractor licence. Plumbing and electrical work must be done by licensed plumbers and electricians, regardless of the job value.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does kitchen installation cost in NSW?

Cabinet installation alone commonly costs about $1,800 to $4,500 depending on size and complexity, and flat pack installs are often quoted around $600 to $2,000. Plumbing, electrical, tiling and appliance connection are usually extra. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### Is installation included in a custom kitchen quote?

With most custom joiners, yes. A supply-and-install quote normally covers cabinets, doors, hardware, scribing, kickboards and sometimes benchtop templating. It usually excludes plumbing, electrical, tiling, painting and appliance installation, so check the inclusions list.

### Why do flat pack kitchens cost more to install than expected?

Flat pack cabinets must be assembled, levelled on uneven floors, scribed to walls that are rarely straight and joined into runs before the benchtop goes on. That labour is the same skill a custom install needs. Add plumbing and electrical, and the total can approach a custom supply-and-install price.

### Do I need a licensed tradesperson to install a kitchen in NSW?

Residential building work valued over $5,000 including labour and materials needs a contractor licence in NSW. Plumbing and electrical work must always be done by licensed plumbers and electricians. Ask every installer for their licence number and check it with Service NSW.

### How long does a kitchen installation take?

A standard custom kitchen is usually installed in two to four days, with the stone benchtop templated after the cabinets go in and fitted one to two weeks later. Plumbing and electrical fit-off follow the benchtop. The whole room is usually back in use within about two to three weeks of the install start.
