# Built In Desk Sydney: Study Nook Ideas, Sizes and Costs

In Sydney, space is the most expensive thing in the house. A built-in desk turns an unused alcove, a hallway end or a corner of the living room into a proper place to work. This guide covers built in desk Sydney ideas for apartments, terraces and family homes, with ergonomic sizes, costs and the questions to ask before you commission one.

## Sizes that work

Ergonomics matter even in a small nook. Desky's calculator puts a comfortable [desk height for most adults at about 635 to 700 mm](https://desky.com.au/pages/desk-height-calculator), and ErgoExperts recommends at least [650 mm of clear knee width](https://ergoexperts.com.au/what-is-the-correct-desk-size/).

| Dimension | Practical guide |
| --- | --- |
| Desk height | About 635–700 mm, set for the main user |
| Desk depth | 500–600 mm for a laptop, 600–700 mm for a monitor |
| Nook width | 900–1,200 mm for one person |
| Knee clearance | At least 650 mm wide, no drawers overhead |
| Shelf above desk | Clear the monitor and allow head room |
| Chair space | Room for the chair to roll back fully |

## Hiding the mess

A study nook in a living space needs to look good when you are not working.

- **Closing doors.** Bi-fold or pocket doors hide the desk at the end of the day.
- **Cable channels.** A vertical channel behind a panel carries power and data.
- **Desk grommets.** Neat cable exit points for laptops and lamps.
- **Closed cupboards.** For printers, routers and paper.
- **Drawer dividers.** Small items stay sorted.
- **LED strip lighting.** Under-shelf lights reduce glare and look good on video calls.

## Strata and access

Many Sydney installs are in strata buildings. Before install day we check:

- Lift booking and protection requirements.
- Approved working hours and noise rules.
- Parking for a van and loading access.
- Building manager contact and insurance certificates.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Building the desk too deep for the space, which narrows walkways.
- Forgetting a power point at desk height.
- Placing the desk facing a bright window with glare on the screen.
- Fixed shelves that do not fit your books or files.
- No ventilation in closed equipment cupboards.

## Children and shared study spaces

Families often want one nook that works for school and work. A long desk for two, lower open shelves for children, and a lockable drawer for the adult's work items works well. Adjustable chairs and footrests help children sit at an adult desk height. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a built-in desk cost in Sydney?

A compact built-in study nook in NSW commonly costs around $4,000 to $5,500, and a single-wall desk with storage around $5,500 to $8,000. Larger two-person workspaces often reach $8,000 to $12,000. Sydney access and parking can add to the install cost. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### What is the minimum space for a study nook?

A usable study nook can fit into an alcove of about 900 to 1,200 mm wide with a desk around 500 to 600 mm deep. Allow at least 650 mm of clear knee width and room for the chair to roll back.

### Can a built-in desk go into a strata apartment?

Usually yes, because built-in joinery is generally fixed to internal walls. Check your strata by-laws for work hours, lift bookings and any rules about fixings to common walls before installation.

### What finish is best for a built-in desk?

Laminate and melamine are durable and affordable for desktops. 2-pac painted doors suit modern and Hamptons homes, and timber veneer adds warmth. For heavy use, a laminate or compact laminate desktop resists scratches best.

### Do you build study nooks for children?

Yes. A family study nook can have a long desk for two, lower shelves for books, and adjustable or replaceable parts as children grow. Plan for good lighting and power for laptops and lamps.
