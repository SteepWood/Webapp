# How Long Does Kitchen Installation Take? A Day-by-Day Guide

How long does kitchen installation take? It is one of the first questions families ask, because living without a kitchen is the hardest part of any renovation. The short answer: the cabinets usually go in over two to four days, but the full process, from strip-out to a working kitchen with benchtops, splashback and appliances, commonly runs over two to four weeks.

This guide walks through the process day by day, based on how SteepWood installs custom kitchens built in our Newcastle workshop across Newcastle, the Central Coast and Sydney.

## The whole timeline at a glance

| Stage | Typical time | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design, measure and approval | 2–4 weeks | Kitchen company and client |
| Manufacture | 4–8 weeks | Workshop |
| Strip-out and preparation | 1–2 days | Installer, plumber, electrician |
| Cabinet installation | 2–4 days | Installer |
| Benchtop templating | 1 day | Benchtop fabricator |
| Benchtop fabrication | 1–2 weeks | Benchtop fabricator |
| Benchtop install | 1 day | Benchtop fabricator |
| Splashback | 1–3 days | Tiler or glazier |
| Plumbing and electrical fit-off | 1–2 days | Licensed trades |
| Final adjustments and handover | 1 day | Installer |

Times vary with kitchen size, benchtop material and site conditions. For the months before installation, see our [kitchen renovation lead times](/blog/kitchen-renovation-timeline-lead-times-nsw) guide.

## Before day one: preparation

A smooth installation starts weeks earlier:

- **Final check measure** after any walls move or plaster is replaced.
- **Appliances chosen** with model numbers, so cut-outs and cabinets match.
- **Services planned:** sink, dishwasher, cooktop, oven and rangehood positions.
- **Switchboard checked:** induction cooktops and new circuits may need an upgrade, commonly around [$1,500 to $3,000](https://kitchenquote.com.au/costs/kitchen-installation-cost/).
- **Temporary kitchen set up** in the laundry or dining room.

## Days 1–2: strip-out and rough-in

The old kitchen is removed, walls and floor are checked, and the plumber and electrician move or add services. This is when hidden problems appear, such as water damage behind the old sink or uneven floors. Allow a small contingency for these. Demolition and disposal of an old kitchen is commonly priced around [$800 to $1,800](https://buildpilot.com.au/learn/kitchens).

If your home was built before 1990, check for asbestos before strip-out. [SafeWork NSW](https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/hazards-a-z/asbestos) requires licensed removal for certain asbestos work.

## Days 3–5: cabinet installation

This is when the kitchen takes shape. The installer:

1. Sets out a level line around the room.
2. Fits base cabinets on adjustable legs or a plinth frame, levelled and fixed to the wall.
3. Fits tall cabinets, such as pantry and oven towers.
4. Fits overhead cabinets.
5. Fits panels, fillers and the island.
6. Hangs doors and fits drawers, ready for adjustment later.

Custom cabinets built to measure usually go in faster than flat pack, because there is little on-site cutting. Cabinet installation on its own is commonly priced around [$1,800 to $4,500](https://kitchenquote.com.au/costs/kitchen-installation-cost/), and with a custom kitchen it is normally included in the supply-and-install price.

## Day 6: benchtop templating

Once the cabinets are fixed, the benchtop fabricator templates the tops. Templating after the cabinets are in place gives exact measurements for walls, corners and cut-outs. Have your sink, tap and cooktop on site or their specifications confirmed by this day.

## Weeks 2–3: benchtop fabrication

This is the waiting period. Stone, porcelain and sintered benchtops are cut and finished in the fabricator's workshop, commonly over one to two weeks. Laminate and timber tops can be faster. Engineered stone is banned in Australia, so new kitchens use [alternatives such as natural stone, porcelain and sintered surfaces](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/hazards/silica/engineered-stone-ban). Our [benchtop guide](/blog/benchtop-guide-engineered-stone-ban-nsw) compares them.

## Benchtop install day

The benchtop is lifted in, levelled, joined and sealed. Sinks are usually fitted at the same time for undermount styles.

## Splashback, plumbing and electrical fit-off

| Task | Typical time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiled splashback | 1–3 days | Tiling, then grout and cure |
| Glass splashback | Measure then fit, often a week apart | Measured after benchtop |
| Stone or porcelain splashback | Templated with or after the benchtop | Often fitted with the benchtop |
| Plumbing fit-off | Half to one day | Sink, tap, dishwasher |
| Electrical fit-off | Half to one day | Cooktop, oven, rangehood, power points |

Plumbing and electrical work must be done by licensed trades. You can [check a builder or tradesperson licence](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/check-a-builder-or-tradesperson-licence) with Service NSW.

## Handover

On the last day, the installer adjusts doors and drawers, fits handles, kicks and any final panels, cleans up and walks you through the care of your finishes and hardware.

## What slows installation down

| Problem | How to avoid it |
| --- | --- |
| Walls out of square | Accurate check measure, scribe panels |
| Uneven floors | Adjustable legs and plinths |
| Late appliances | Order early and confirm delivery dates |
| Changes after templating | Lock in sink and tap before templating |
| Hidden water damage | Allow contingency and time |
| Switchboard upgrade needed | Electrician check before work starts |

## Tips for living through it

- Set up a temporary kitchen with a microwave, kettle and portable cooktop.
- Plan meals for two to four weeks without a full kitchen.
- Protect floors and doorways along the path to the kitchen.
- Keep pets and children away from the work area.
- Agree a daily start and finish time with the installer.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does kitchen installation take?

Installing the cabinets for a standard custom kitchen usually takes two to four days. The full sequence, including benchtop templating and fitting, splashback, plumbing and electrical fit-off, commonly runs over two to four weeks from strip-out to a working kitchen.

### Why is there a gap between cabinets and benchtops?

Stone, porcelain and sintered benchtops are templated after the cabinets are fixed so the measurements are exact. Fabrication then commonly takes one to two weeks before the benchtop is installed.

### Can I use my kitchen during installation?

Usually not. Once the old kitchen is removed, there is no working sink or cooktop until benchtops are fitted and plumbing and electrical fit-off are done. Many families set up a temporary kitchen in the laundry or dining room.

### Who coordinates plumbers and electricians?

It depends on your contract. Some kitchen companies coordinate all trades, while others supply and install cabinets only. Confirm in writing who books each trade and who is responsible for delays.

### What slows a kitchen installation down?

Common delays include out-of-square walls, uneven floors, hidden water damage, late appliance deliveries, changes after templating and switchboard upgrades. A clear site measure and early appliance selection reduce most of these.
