# Kitchen Installers Central Coast: What to Expect in 2026

The Central Coast is one of the busiest renovation markets in NSW, and kitchens lead the way. Finding kitchen installers Central Coast homeowners can trust is about more than price. It is about who measures, who builds, who installs and who comes back if a drawer needs adjusting. This guide explains how installation works, what Coast homes need and what it costs.

SteepWood designs and builds kitchens in our Newcastle workshop and installs them across the Central Coast with our own team.

ABS data shows the Central Coast approved [$166.0 million of residential alterations and additions in 2025–26](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/building-and-construction/building-approvals-australia/jun-2026/87310do005_202606.xlsx), one of the highest totals in NSW. ## Coastal conditions and kitchen materials

Humidity is the main enemy of kitchen cabinetry near the coast. We recommend:

- Moisture-resistant board under the sink and around the dishwasher.
- Edges sealed on all sides, including hidden edges.
- Quality hinges and runners that resist corrosion.
- A rangehood ducted outside to reduce steam.
- Gaps and seals around the sink cut-out.

Laminate and 2-pac both perform well on the Coast when edges are sealed properly. Our [kitchen finishes guide](/blog/2pac-laminate-timber-veneer-kitchen-finishes-nsw) compares them.

## How a Coast kitchen install runs

1. **Measure and design** in your home.
2. **Approve drawings** and finishes.
3. **Manufacture** in our workshop.
4. **Demolition** of the old kitchen, if included.
5. **Install** cabinets, panels and kickboards.
6. **Benchtop templating**, then fitting.
7. **Plumbing and electrical fit-off.**
8. **Final adjustments** and handover.

Because we travel from Newcastle, we plan each Coast install as a block of days so the work runs continuously.

## Preparing your home for installation day

A little preparation makes installation faster and less stressful.

- Clear the kitchen, pantry and nearby benches completely.
- Set up a temporary kitchen in the laundry or dining room with a kettle, microwave and fridge.
- Make sure there is parking close to the house for the van and trailer, especially on narrow streets in Terrigal and Avoca.
- Tell neighbours if access or noise may affect them.
- Keep pets secured and children away from the work area.
- Have your appliance manuals ready, because cut-outs and clearances must match the models.
- Confirm when the plumber and electrician are booked so the kitchen is usable as soon as possible.

On hillside homes with long stairs, tell us at the measure so we can plan how cabinets and benchtops will be carried in. That avoids damage and keeps the day on schedule.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do kitchen installers charge on the Central Coast?

Cabinet installation commonly costs around $1,800 to $4,500, with plumbing, electrical, appliances and tiling usually extra. With a custom kitchen, installation is normally included in the supply-and-install price. These are indicative 2026 ranges, not a quote.

### Do you install kitchens from Newcastle on the Central Coast?

Yes. SteepWood is based in Newcastle and regularly installs custom kitchens across the Central Coast, from Wyong and Tuggerah to Gosford, Erina, Terrigal and the Peninsula.

### What board is best for a coastal kitchen?

Moisture-resistant board for sink and dishwasher cabinets, sealed edges and quality hinges and runners all help in humid coastal homes. Laminate and 2-pac both work well when edges are properly sealed.

### How long does a Central Coast kitchen installation take?

A standard custom kitchen is usually installed over a few days, with the stone or porcelain benchtop templated after the cabinets are fixed and fitted later. Plumbing and electrical fit-off follow the benchtop.

### Do I need a licensed kitchen installer?

In NSW, residential building work over $5,000 including labour and materials needs a contractor licence. Plumbing and electrical work must be done by licensed trades. Ask for licence numbers and check them with Service NSW.
